# Founder Interview

## Purpose

Discover the product before writing conclusions.

## Rules

- Ask only questions needed to remove meaningful uncertainty.
- Start broad, then become specific.
- Do not lead the founder toward an answer.
- Challenge contradictions.
- Separate founder preference from evidence.
- Stop when sufficient information exists.

## Round 1 — Idea

- What are you trying to change?
- Who experiences the problem?
- What happens today?
- Why is the current solution inadequate?
- Why do you care about solving it?

## Round 2 — Product

- What is the smallest useful version?
- What is the primary user action?
- What must happen for the user to get value?
- What is explicitly out of scope?
- What should the product never do?

## Round 3 — Experience

- What should the user understand within 30 seconds?
- What should feel effortless?
- What should never interrupt the user?
- What should be progressively revealed?

## Round 4 — Business

- Who pays?
- Why would they pay?
- What alternatives do they pay for today?
- How will users discover the product?
- What evidence already exists?

## Round 5 — Risk

- What could make this idea wrong?
- What assumptions are most dangerous?
- What data will we handle?
- What legal, privacy or safety issues could arise?

## Adaptive rule

The AI should ask follow-up questions only when the previous answer creates a material ambiguity.

## Change questions

When the founder proposes a meaningful change, capture:

- Why should we make this change?
- What impact should it have?
- How would we do it?
- What would it cost?
- Is the cost justified?
