Today we shipped Trove V2. It is, in most respects, an entirely new product.

## Why a rewrite

V1 served 200+ users and proved the thesis. V2 is what the product wanted to be all along — built on a stack we can scale on for years, designed end-to-end with the same eye we'd give a finished product, and shipped with the rigor of a funded company. Trove is still a one-person studio. The output is what matters.

## What's new

- Next.js 15 App Router. Faster, lighter, edge-aware.
- New design system: FT × Apple. Cream, gold, Georgia serif.
- Supabase SSR auth + RLS on every table.
- Stripe billing wired end-to-end.
- AI insights moved to a Gemini proxy with prompt sanitization.
- Full marketing site, docs, blog with MDX + RSS + structured data.
- Admin panel for users, billing, feature flags, analytics.
- Tests + CI: Vitest, Playwright, axe a11y, CodeQL, Lighthouse.

## What we removed

- Plausible (replaced by PostHog).
- In-memory rate limiting (replaced by Upstash sliding window).
- The "lab" page (it served its purpose; the dashboard now does that work).
