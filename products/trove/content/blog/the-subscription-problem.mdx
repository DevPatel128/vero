When we shipped subscription detection to the V1 pilot, the average user found $84/month of recurring charges they didn't know about. Eighty-four dollars. Every month. Forever.

## Why this happens

Three reasons:

1. **The free trial trap.** You signed up in 2023. They charged you in 2024. You never noticed.
2. **The bundle blur.** Apple One, Amazon Prime, Adobe Creative Cloud — bundles paper over what you actually use.
3. **The shame discount.** Some apps deliberately hide cancel buttons because they know you'll wait three more months before you actually cancel.

## What Trove does

When we detect a recurring charge from the same merchant for three or more months, we flag it as a subscription. You see:

- Cadence (monthly, yearly).
- Last charge date.
- Next renewal.
- Spend over the last year.

You decide what to do. We don't auto-cancel anything (yet).

## A test

Open your bank statement. Look at the last 90 days. Count the charges you'd be surprised to still be paying for.

You don't need Trove to do this. But you'll do it once, feel virtuous, and then never do it again. Trove does it every Sunday at 6am while you sleep.
