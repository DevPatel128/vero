## Encryption

- TLS 1.3 in transit.
- AES-256 at rest (Postgres + storage).
- Row-level security on every table.
- Service-role keys server-only, rotated quarterly.

## What we never store

- Bank login credentials. Plaid handles auth; we receive read-only tokens.
- Credit card numbers. Stripe handles billing.
- Full payment account numbers. Only last-4 masks.

## Deleting your data

`/settings` → Delete account. Within 30 days, all data — including backups — is purged. We email a confirmation.

## Reporting a vulnerability

`security@trove.vroelabs.com`. We respond within 24 hours and credit researchers publicly.
