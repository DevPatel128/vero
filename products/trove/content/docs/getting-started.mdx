## 1. Sign up

Email or Google. We bring you straight to a four-step onboarding.

## 2. Connect or import

Two paths:

- **Plaid** — read-only OAuth to U.S. banks. Takes 30 seconds.
- **CSV** — drop any CSV from your bank or accountant. We auto-detect columns.

## 3. Set your monthly income

Used for cash-flow math. You can edit anytime.

## 4. See your dashboard

You're done. Net position, cash flow, top categories, financial health score — all on one screen.

## Next steps

- Set a budget for the categories you care about (`/budgets`).
- Audit your subscriptions (`/subscriptions`).
- Ask the AI: "Where did my money go in April?"
