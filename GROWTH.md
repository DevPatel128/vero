## Go-to-market plan
- **Market:** Bengaluru only, five zones (Whitefield, HSR Layout, Koramangala, Sarjapur, Electronic City). Density beats reach.
- **Channels:** (1) campus ambassadors, one per campus, rewarded on retained users with verified work, not signups; (2) startup and creative communities: co-working spaces (WeWork, Bhive, 91springboard named as examples), founder content, hiring panels, outreach to founders who cannot afford recruiters; (3) worker word of mouth: shareable record link, PDF or badge, milestone share cards.
- **Referrals:** rewarded on referred users' completed signed jobs, not on count. The live waitlist moves a person up 5 places per referral.
- **Never:** fake scarcity, re-engagement spam, hidden fees, forced upsells, fabricated metrics, paid placement in ranking.
- **Targets (PROJECTION):** month 1: 250 workers, 50 businesses, 30+ signed jobs, 5 ambassadors. Month 3: 1,000, 200, 250+, 15. Month 6: 3,000, 600, 1,000+, 30+. Activation: 35% first-job completion, median under 14 days to first record, 70%+ profile completion. Quality: disputes under 10%, 70%+ resolved at tier 1, fraud under 1% of accounts. Retention: 25%+ repeat hires within 90 days, 60%+ worker retention. The smaller `Documents/6` bar (200+ records, 50+ businesses in 90 days) was rejected as the target on 2026-09-29.
- **Market sizing (UNVERIFIED, no citation; label "internal estimate" if used):** TAM $400B+ India gig economy by 2030; 90M+ workers; 50M+ SMBs; SAM 1M+ workers and 50,000+ SMBs in Bengaluru; SOM in 24 months 30,000+ workers, 5,000+ businesses, ₹50 to 100 Cr annual escrow volume.
