## Who decides what

Dev Patel (founder/owner) holds final authority over product, legal, financial, privacy, security-exception and production decisions (`00_START_HERE/README.md` "Authority"). There is no product team yet — see `00_START_HERE/ORGANIZATION.md`.

## How a capability moves from idea to shipped

`DISCOVERY.md` → `02_PRODUCT/INTERVIEW.md` (if founder intent is ambiguous) → `03_RESEARCH/RESEARCH.md` (evidence) → `08_DECISIONS/` entry (why/impact/how/cost/justification, per `01_PRINCIPLES/PRINCIPLES.md`) → added to `PRODUCT.md`'s capabilities table and `ROADMAP.md` → built, tested, reviewed → human approval → deployed → observed (`06_OPERATIONS/OBSERVABILITY.md`) → learned from → `08_DECISIONS/` updated.

## Prioritization

`PRODUCT.md`'s core capabilities table carries a `Priority` column (P0/P2/P2+/P5) tied to `Documents/13`'s phase scope, not a formal scoring framework (no RICE/WSJF or similar documented). Non-P0 work is explicitly out of scope until its phase's guardrail metrics are met (`ROADMAP.md`).

## Cadence

Not defined in the source. No sprint, release-train or planning cadence is documented — Phase 0 is a single founder plus an AI assistant, not a team with a cadence to define yet (`00_START_HERE/ORGANIZATION.md`).
