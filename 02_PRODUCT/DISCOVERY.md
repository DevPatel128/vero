## Problem space

There is no reliable way to answer "did this person actually do what they say they did?" Resumes are unverifiable, gig-platform ratings measure satisfaction not quality, portfolios can be faked in minutes with AI, and reputation built on one platform does not transfer to the next. Full argument: `THESIS.md`.

## Opportunity framing

Two groups, one shared unmet need (proof of capability that transfers between contexts):

- **Workers/professionals** who need to prove capability they cannot currently show through a resume or portfolio.
- **Businesses** who need to hire without guessing, at a lower cost than existing gig platforms' commission (`PRODUCT.md`'s core capabilities table).
