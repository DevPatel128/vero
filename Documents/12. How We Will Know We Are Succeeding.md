# How We Will Know We Are Succeeding

This document defines what success means for VERO — at every stage, in plain numbers, with no fluff. If the team has to ask "are we doing well?", these are the metrics that answer.

---

## The Single Most Important Metric — The North Star

**Percentage of active workers with at least 3 signed work records in their first 90 days.**

This single number captures everything that matters. It means:
- People are signing up (acquisition)
- People are completing real jobs (activation)
- Jobs are being signed by both sides (verification works)
- Workers are sticking around long enough to build a track record (retention)
- The platform is delivering value (product-market fit)

If this number is growing, VERO is winning. If it is flat or falling, something is fundamentally wrong and needs to be diagnosed before any other initiative.

**Targets:**
- Month 3: 15% of active workers have 3+ signed records
- Month 6: 25% of active workers have 3+ signed records
- Month 12: 40% of active workers have 3+ signed records

## Product-Market Fit Signal

VERO uses the **Sean Ellis test** for product-market fit:

Ask active workers and businesses: "How would you feel if you could no longer use VERO?"

Options:
- Very disappointed
- Somewhat disappointed
- Not disappointed

**The PMF threshold:** 40% or more answer "very disappointed".

If VERO hits 40% on this question among workers who have completed at least 2 jobs, that is strong evidence that the product is genuinely valuable. Below 40%, the product needs more iteration before scaling.

## What VERO Will Not Track

Some metrics other startups obsess over — but they are not relevant for VERO and can be actively misleading.

- **Vanity signups** — sign-ups without verification or first-job completion mean nothing
- **Time on platform** — VERO does not want users scrolling forever; it wants them completing real work
- **Engagement at any cost** — pushing users to come back constantly is the opposite of what VERO is for
- **Daily active users (DAU)** — not relevant for a transactional trust platform; weekly or monthly is more meaningful
- **App store rankings** (Phase 1) — no native app yet
- **Social media followers** — vanity metric; reach matters less than trust

## How These Metrics Get Reviewed

- **Weekly:** Acquisition, activation, current week's job activity (team review)
- **Monthly:** Full dashboard, retention cohorts, dispute analysis (team + advisors)
- **Quarterly:** Strategic review against phase targets, expansion readiness, PMF signal (full company + investors)

If a metric falls significantly behind target for two consecutive review periods, it triggers a structured investigation: what changed, what's the root cause, what's the corrective action.

---

## Defining Failure

Just as important as defining success.

VERO is not succeeding if, after 6 months of operation:
- Fewer than 20% of active workers have completed any verified job
- Dispute rate is consistently above 15%
- Repeat hire rate is below 10%
- Fraud or gaming incidents are common
- Workers do not feel their records are genuinely valuable

If any of these are true, the product is broken in a fundamental way and needs major rework — not just a marketing push.
