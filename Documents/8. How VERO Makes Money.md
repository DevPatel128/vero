# How VERO Makes Money

VERO's business model is built on a simple principle: the people who do the work should never pay to build their record. Monetization comes from the side that benefits most from verified trust — businesses that hire.

---

## The Core Model

| Who | What they pay | Amount |
|-----|--------------|--------|
| **Workers** | Nothing | Free — always |
| **Businesses — subscription** | Monthly or annual plan | Tiered SaaS pricing (see below) |
| **Escrow fee** | Both sides contribute | 5% of paid job value |

---

## Workers Are Always Free

Workers — the people who build their proof-of-work identity on VERO — pay nothing. Ever. This is a non-negotiable part of what VERO is.

The reasoning: if workers had to pay to build their own record, VERO would be charging people for the right to prove they did their job. That is backwards. The worker's record is theirs. VERO is the infrastructure that makes it verifiable. Infrastructure should not charge the people whose work creates its value.

This also matters strategically: free access for workers means fast adoption, large supply, and a valuable pool of verified talent for businesses to hire from.

## Business Subscription — SaaS Model

Businesses pay a monthly or annual subscription to access the platform and hire from the verified worker pool.

**Planned tiers (Phase 1 — India pricing):**

| Tier | Who it is for | What it includes | Price (approx.) |
|------|--------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Starter** | Solo hirer, small business, 1–2 hires/month | Basic job postings, access to verified worker pool, standard support | ₹499–999/month |
| **Growth** | SMBs, agencies, cafés, studios — 5–15 hires/month | More job slots, priority applicant access, hire analytics, faster support | ₹2,499–4,999/month |
| **Studio** | Larger businesses, repeat hiring, 20+ hires/month | Unlimited jobs, team access, advanced hiring analytics, dedicated support | Custom pricing |

Note: Exact pricing will be validated with early business users before the public launch. These are indicative ranges.

## Escrow Fee — 5% on Paid Work

For every paid job processed through VERO's escrow system, a 5% fee is charged and shared between the worker (2.5%) and the business (2.5%). This fee covers payment processing, escrow holding, dispute support, and the infrastructure that makes the transaction secure.

**Why 5%?** Urban Company charges workers up to 25% commission. Fiverr and Upwork charge 20%+. VERO charges 5% — split across both sides — because the goal is to replace those platforms, not to replicate their extractive economics.

**How escrow works:** The business funds the agreed amount into escrow via Razorpay before the job starts. The money is held safely until both sides sign the completion. On signing, the worker receives the agreed amount minus the 2.5% fee. VERO retains its 2.5%.

## Future Revenue Streams (Phase 3+)

As VERO grows and the trust graph matures, additional revenue layers become possible:

- **Advanced analytics for businesses** — hiring intelligence, worker performance trends, category insights
- **Verified credential exports** — workers pay a small fee to generate a formally certified portable credential
- **Enterprise access tier** — large companies and institutions that want API access to VERO's trust data
- **Premium hiring tools** — featured job listings, applicant filtering, team hiring workflows

None of these are part of Phase 1. They are mentioned here so the business model logic is clear — the goal is not to be dependent on a single revenue source long-term.

---

## Why This Model Works

**For workers:** Free access means rapid adoption. A large, high-quality worker pool is what makes businesses want to pay.

**For businesses:** Subscription pricing is predictable. They pay for access to trust infrastructure, not per-transaction fees that add up fast. The 5% escrow fee is transparent and fair.

**For VERO:** Subscription revenue is recurring and scalable. Escrow fees grow with transaction volume. The two streams reinforce each other — more active hirers means more escrow transactions, which means more revenue from both lines.

**The comparison:** Urban Company takes 25% from every worker transaction. Fiverr takes 20%. VERO takes 5% on escrow (split between both sides) plus a business subscription. For workers, this is dramatically better. For businesses, the subscription model gives them predictable costs and access to a better-verified talent pool.

---

## Launch Pricing Strategy

For the first cohort (Phase 1), early businesses will be offered free or heavily discounted access. The goal is to build density — enough active hirers to create a healthy job board for workers — before charging at full price.

Free early access is not charity. It is the cost of building a two-sided marketplace where both sides have a reason to be present.
