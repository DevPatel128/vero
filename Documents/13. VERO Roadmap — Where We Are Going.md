# VERO Roadmap — Where We Are Going

This document explains the long-term roadmap for VERO in plain language. It is organised by phase, not by feature list. Each phase has a clear goal, a clear definition of "done", and a clear reason for what comes next.

The roadmap is deliberately conservative. VERO does not expand into a new phase until the previous one is genuinely stable. This is the difference between a product that grows healthily and one that collapses under its own ambition.

---

## Phase 0 — Pre-Launch (Now)

**Goal:** Build the foundation. Have the marketing website live. Build the application.

**Where we are:**
- Marketing website is live at vero.work (or equivalent) — explains VERO clearly, collects waitlist signups, educates early audience
- The strategic foundation is documented (this folder of documents)
- The framework for building the company is in place
- The web application is being built

**Done when:** the application is functional, tested, and ready for limited internal use.

---

## Phase 1 — Bengaluru Pilot (Months 1–6 of launch)

**Goal:** Prove that the core mechanic works. Workers and businesses in Bengaluru complete real, signed, escrow-protected jobs — and the trust system holds up under real use.

**What VERO does in this phase:**
- Onboard verified workers across the five priority Bengaluru zones
- Onboard verified businesses (cafés, studios, startups, SMBs)
- Activate the six launch career categories
- Run the dual-signature work record system with real jobs and real money
- Handle real disputes (this is the most important test of the trust system)
- Iterate the product based on actual user behavior

**What VERO deliberately does not do in this phase:**
- Expand to other cities
- Add new career categories beyond the launch six
- Build a native mobile app
- Launch enterprise features
- Run heavy paid marketing

**Phase is done when:**
- 3,000+ verified workers and 600+ verified businesses are active in Bengaluru
- 1,000+ signed work records have been created
- Dispute rate is under 10% and resolution rates are high
- Repeat hire rate is at least 25%
- The product is stable, fast, and reliable
- The team has clear conviction (and data) that the mechanic works

---

## Phase 2 — Category and Local Depth (Months 6–12)

**Goal:** Deepen what VERO offers in Bengaluru before expanding geographically.

**What VERO does:**
- Add new career categories that build on the foundation: elder assistance, pet care, beauty and wellness, hospitality, tutoring, home repair, creator economy, digital business support
- Roll out a native mobile app (iOS and Android) for workers on the go
- Launch verified milestones and shareable achievement cards
- Build city reputation pages and neighborhood-level density features
- Activate ambassador program across 20+ campuses
- Improve trust signals based on real Phase 1 data

**What VERO does not do yet:**
- Move into other cities at scale
- Launch blockchain-based credentials
- Enterprise-tier features

**Phase is done when:**
- Categories are well-supported with real density (not just listings)
- The mobile app has parity with the web experience for workers
- Trust system is mature and well-tuned based on Phase 1 learnings
- Strong product-market fit signal: 40%+ "very disappointed" on the Sean Ellis test

---

## Phase 3 — City Expansion (Months 12–24)

**Goal:** Take what works in Bengaluru and replicate it in carefully chosen new cities.

**Expansion priority order:**
1. Delhi NCR
2. Mumbai
3. Pune
4. Hyderabad
5. Chennai

**What VERO does:**
- Open one new city at a time, with local ambassadors and local business networks
- Adapt to local hiring norms and language preferences
- Build city-specific reputation layers
- Maintain Bengaluru as the densest, most mature market

**The expansion rule:** No new city is opened until the previous one is stable. "Stable" means: enough density to be self-sustaining, repeat hire rate is healthy, and the dispute rate is low.

**What VERO does not do:**
- Open many cities at once
- Spread thin across regions to chase headline numbers
- Compromise on verification standards to grow faster

**Phase is done when:**
- Three to four major Indian cities are running healthy trust networks
- VERO is recognisable as the credible verified-hire platform in those markets

---

## Phase 4 — Trust and Reputation Maturity (Months 18–30, overlapping with Phase 3)

**Goal:** Make VERO records the most credible professional credential in India.

**What VERO does:**
- Deepen profile depth — verified work history connects to verified skills, verified specialisations, verified mentorships
- Build the recruiter and HR product layer — companies use VERO records as a primary hiring signal
- Partner with educational institutions to verify learning outcomes alongside work outcomes
- Build the "portable proof-of-work credential" — exportable, shareable, recognised
- Mature client credibility tracking — businesses also have trust scores that workers can see before applying

**Phase is done when:**
- Major employers in India recognise VERO records as a meaningful hiring signal
- Workers can carry their VERO credentials into job interviews, partnerships, and opportunities outside the platform with confidence

---

## Phase 5 — Blockchain Extension (Months 24–36)

**Goal:** Make VERO records permanently portable, even beyond VERO itself.

**What VERO does:**
- Anchor signed work records on a low-fee, mainstream-friendly blockchain (likely Polygon or a similar L2)
- Issue verifiable digital credentials that can be checked by anyone, anywhere, without VERO being online
- Enable smart-contract escrow as an option for tech-savvy users
- Support stablecoin payments (USDC, USDT) for cross-border hires
- Build the "reputation passport" — a worker's full verified history, portable across platforms and borders

**What VERO does not do:**
- Launch a speculative token
- Become a crypto-first brand
- Make blockchain visible in everyday UX (it works behind the scenes)

**Phase is done when:**
- VERO records are blockchain-anchored and verifiable independently
- Workers can use their VERO credentials in employment contexts globally
- Cross-border verified hiring is functional through VERO

---

## Phase 6 — Wider Expansion (Year 3+)

**Goal:** Scale beyond India where it makes strategic sense.

**Likely first international markets:**
- Southeast Asia (Indonesia, Philippines, Vietnam) — similar trust gaps in hiring, large young workforces
- Middle East (UAE, Saudi Arabia) — high concentration of expatriate workers needing verifiable history

**Phase is done when:**
- VERO is operating as a multi-country trust infrastructure
- The core mechanics work across regulatory regimes and cultural contexts

---

## Phase 7 — Ecosystem Growth (Year 4+)

**Goal:** Build VERO from a product into an ecosystem.

**What this means:**
- Mentor network: experienced professionals endorsing rising workers
- Employer network: companies that pre-commit to hiring from VERO's verified pool
- Education partners: institutions that integrate VERO records into student credentials
- City ambassador networks across countries
- Portable reputation integrations with other professional platforms

**This phase is open-ended and depends on what the market needs by then.**

---

## The Rules That Govern the Roadmap

These rules constrain how VERO advances through phases. They are non-negotiable.

1. **A phase is not done until trust is stable.** If disputes spike or fraud incidents are common, the phase is not done — regardless of growth numbers.

2. **Quality beats expansion.** Adding categories, cities, or features that the team cannot support well damages the brand. Better to do less, better.

3. **Workers always come first.** Every roadmap decision is checked against: does this help workers build a more valuable verified record? If not, it does not happen.

4. **No premature complexity.** Architecture, features, and partnerships are added only when the product genuinely needs them. Speculative building burns money and slows the team.

5. **Theme lock holds.** The brand, the tone, the design system, and the trust principles do not change phase-to-phase. Only the surface area grows.

---

## What VERO Will Never Become

To stay focused, it is useful to declare what VERO will never become — no matter how tempting:

- A bidding marketplace
- A social network with feeds and follows
- A platform that charges workers to be on it
- A black-box trust score that users cannot understand
- An aggressive sales-driven enterprise tool
- A crypto speculation platform
- A platform that owns the worker's reputation

These are clear lines. The roadmap moves around them, not through them.
