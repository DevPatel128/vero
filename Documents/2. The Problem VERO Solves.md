# The Problem VERO Solves

The hiring and freelancing system is broken in a very specific way — not because people are dishonest, but because the tools we use make it impossible to tell who has actually done the work.

---

## The Five Core Problems

### Problem 1 — Resumes say whatever the person wants

Anyone can write anything on a resume. "Led a team of 10." "Increased revenue by 40%." "Built the entire backend." Most hiring managers know these claims are unverifiable — and yet they have no better option. The result is that good work goes unseen and bad hires keep happening, costing time and money that most businesses cannot afford to waste.

### Problem 2 — Gig platforms race to the bottom

Fiverr, Upwork, and their clones push everyone to compete on price and response time. The person who bids lowest and replies fastest wins — not the person who does the best work. Good workers are priced out. Clients get exactly what they paid for, or less. Neither side is well served by a system designed to maximize transaction volume rather than work quality.

### Problem 3 — Portfolios can be faked in an afternoon

Screenshots can be copied from someone else's work. AI can generate a convincing case study, logo, or code sample in minutes. There is no way to know, just by looking at a portfolio, whether the person showing it actually made any of it. As AI-generated content gets more realistic, this problem gets worse, not better.

### Problem 4 — Star ratings measure satisfaction, not quality

A 4.8 rating on a platform means the client was reasonably happy with the price and speed of delivery. It does not mean the work was excellent. It does not mean the person showed up on time, communicated well, or handled problems professionally. Ratings are a proxy for satisfaction — a weak signal that tells you almost nothing about capability.

### Problem 5 — Your reputation disappears when you leave a platform

Three years of great reviews on one platform count for nothing when you move to another. You start at zero every time. Workers who have built a real track record have no way to take it with them — because the platform owns it, not them. This is the deepest structural problem: the people who do the real work do not own the proof of it.

---

## Who Suffers Most

**Workers** — particularly young people, students, and early-career professionals in India — spend years building skills and doing real work, but cannot prove it in a way that anyone trusts. Their capability is invisible to anyone who does not already know them.

**Businesses** — particularly small and medium businesses, cafés, studios, agencies, and startups in cities like Bengaluru — make hiring decisions with almost no reliable information. Every hire is a risk. Bad hires cost money, time, and sometimes reputation.

**Solo clients** — people who need a plumber, a designer, or a weekend helper — have no safe way to find someone trustworthy. They rely on word of mouth, which is limited, or on platforms whose ratings they cannot fully trust.

---

## What Is Missing

A simple, reliable way to answer one question: **did this person actually do what they say they did?**

Not a rating. Not a resume. Not a portfolio. A signed, verified record — co-signed by the person who hired them — that says: yes, this work happened, it was real, and both sides agree on it.

That is what VERO provides.
