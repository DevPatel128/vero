# VERO Investor Summary

This is a single-document overview for investors. It is written to be readable in 5–7 minutes and covers everything an investor needs to understand the opportunity, the team, and the plan.

---

## The 30-Second Pitch

VERO is building the verified proof-of-work identity layer for India's next workforce. Workers complete real jobs and earn permanent, signed records they own forever. Businesses hire by proof, not presentation. Starting in Bengaluru, expanding across India.

**The shift:** AI is making fake credentials trivially cheap. As a result, verified human-attested work records become scarce — and valuable.

**The business:** Workers are free forever. Businesses pay a subscription plus a 5% escrow fee on paid work. This is dramatically less extractive than the Fiverr (20%) or Urban Company (25%) commission models.

**The moat:** A longitudinal database of dual-signed, verified work records cannot be replicated quickly. Every new signed record makes the network more valuable and harder to compete with.

---

## The Problem

India has 600+ million working-age people. The vast majority cannot prove their professional capability in a way anyone trusts. Resumes are unverifiable. Gig platforms race to the bottom on price. AI-generated portfolios are indistinguishable from real ones. Reputation built on any platform disappears when the worker leaves.

For businesses — particularly the millions of SMBs, cafés, studios, agencies, and early-stage startups — hiring is mostly guesswork. Bad hires are common, costly, and slow growth.

Nobody has built a way to answer one simple question: **did this person actually do what they say they did?**

---

## The Solution

VERO is verified proof-of-work identity infrastructure.

**For workers:**
- Verify identity once
- Browse and apply to verified opportunities (no bidding)
- Complete real work with pre-agreed scope and escrow protection
- Both sides sign — a permanent record is created
- The record belongs to the worker, not to VERO

**For businesses:**
- Verify business identity once
- Post jobs with clear scope and budget
- Hire from workers with already-verified records
- Pay through escrow — protected until completion
- Sign on completion — funds release automatically

**The trust system (ALVED):** Multiple independent signals (completions, repeat clients, dispute history, endorsement quality, consistency over time) combine into a transparent, hard-to-manipulate trust standing.

---

## Why Now

Three converging trends make this the right moment to build VERO:

1. **AI is destroying credential value.** Anyone can generate a convincing portfolio or resume in minutes. Verifiable human-attested work becomes scarce and valuable.

2. **Project-based work is exploding.** More people are freelancing, apprenticing, or working flexibly. They need portable proof of what they have done.

3. **India's workforce is at a structural inflection point.** Massive young population, dense urban ecosystems, rapidly growing services economies — and almost no reliable infrastructure for verified hiring.

## The Business Model

| Stream | Who pays | How much | Why |
|--------|---------|---------|-----|
| Worker access | Workers | Free | Workers should never pay to build their own record |
| Business subscription | Businesses | Starter ₹499–999/mo, Growth ₹2,499–4,999/mo, Studio custom | Recurring SaaS revenue, predictable |
| Escrow fee | Both sides | 5% total (2.5% each) | Covers payment processing, escrow, dispute support |
| Enterprise tier (Phase 3+) | Large enterprises | Custom | API access, advanced analytics, premium hiring tools |

**Comparison to incumbents:**
- Fiverr: 20% commission on workers
- Upwork: 20%+ commission on workers
- Urban Company: 25% commission on workers

VERO charges workers nothing and businesses far less than any of these.

---

## The Competition

VERO does not have direct competitors. The closest comparables are:

- **Fiverr / Upwork** — bidding marketplaces. Wrong model. VERO is not racing to the bottom.
- **LinkedIn** — self-reported claims. VERO is verified proof.
- **Urban Company** — platform-owned worker profiles. VERO gives ownership to workers.
- **Word-of-mouth** — works in tight networks. VERO digitises and scales this trust.

The strategic position: VERO is building a new category — verified proof-of-work identity infrastructure — that does not fully exist yet. The goal is to own this category before anyone else realises it is worth owning.
