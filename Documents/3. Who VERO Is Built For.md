# Who VERO Is Built For

VERO is built for three types of people — workers who need to prove their work, businesses that need to hire by proof, and solo clients who need one trustworthy person for one job.

---

## Type 1 — Workers and Professionals

These are the people who build and use VERO every day.

**Who they are:**
- Students, recent graduates, and early-career professionals (typically 18–28)
- Designers, developers, video editors, writers, marketers
- Skilled trades: electricians, plumbers, carpenters, event staff
- Fitness coaches, personal trainers, yoga instructors
- Chefs, bakers, catering assistants
- Creators, photographers, videographers
- Operators, researchers, growth assistants

**What they have in common:**
- They have done real work, but struggle to prove it to people who do not already know them
- They are ambitious and high-agency — they want to build something real, not just collect titles
- They are frustrated that their actual capability is invisible to potential hirers
- They feel like their work disappears when they leave a platform or a job

**What they need from VERO:**
- A portable, signed history of every verified job they have completed
- A way to build reputation through doing real work, not gaming a platform
- A record that belongs to them — not to any company or platform
- Entry into a network where their execution speaks for itself

**Why they join early:**
"I need to be early. This is the future of how work gets recognized."

---

## Type 2 — Businesses

These are the people who use VERO to find and hire verified workers.

**Who they are:**
- Cafés, restaurants, and food businesses
- Design studios, creative agencies, production companies
- Startups and tech companies (typically early-stage, 2–50 people)
- Event companies and hospitality operations
- Local service businesses (cleaning, home services, wellness)
- Recruiters and HR teams at growing companies

**What they have in common:**
- They have been burned by bad hires — people who looked great on paper but could not do the work
- Screening is expensive and time-consuming, and most of it is based on weak signals
- They would pay more for a hire they could trust — if they had a reliable way to verify capability

**What they need from VERO:**
- Access to workers whose history is already verified — so they spend less time screening
- The ability to see completed work records, client endorsements, and reliability data
- Confidence that the person they hire has actually done what they claim
- A payment system that protects their money until the work is confirmed complete

**Why they join early:**
"I can finally hire by proof, not presentation."

---

## Type 3 — Solo Clients

These are individuals who need one specific thing done — by one reliable person.

**Who they are:**
- Homeowners who need a plumber, electrician, or handyperson
- Families who need a weekend helper or catering support
- Individuals who need a designer, editor, or photographer for a one-time project

**What they need from VERO:**
- A straightforward way to find someone with a real, verified track record
- Confidence that the person has done this kind of work before and was paid fairly for it
- Payment protection — their money is held safely until the job is done

## Who Uses VERO at Each Phase

### Phase 1 (Now — Bengaluru pilot)
- Ambitious students and young professionals in Bengaluru
- Startups and SMBs in Whitefield, HSR Layout, Koramangala, Sarjapur, Electronic City
- First categories: creative, culinary, business operations, fitness, event support

### Phase 2 (After Phase 1 is stable)
- Broader professional categories: elder care, beauty and wellness, hospitality, tutoring
- Recruiters and HR teams discovering verified talent
- Mentors who endorse workers they have supervised

### Phase 3 (Long-term)
- Professionals across India building portable career identities
- Enterprises using VERO as a hiring intelligence layer
- Institutions integrating VERO records into their hiring workflows

---

## Who VERO Is Not Built For

- People who want to game a system or manufacture fake reputation
- Workers who are not interested in being verified or accountable
- Businesses looking for the cheapest possible hire regardless of quality
- Platforms or intermediaries that want to own the worker's reputation data
