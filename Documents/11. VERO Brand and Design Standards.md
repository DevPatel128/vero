# VERO Brand and Design Standards

This document is for anyone who creates anything in VERO's name — designers, writers, marketers, contractors, or future hires. It explains what VERO sounds like, looks like, and feels like — and what it must never become.

---

## What VERO Stands For

**Proof over claims.** This is the single sentence that shapes every decision.

VERO is calm, premium, intelligent, practical, and trustworthy. It is not loud, not hyped, not playful, and not casual. It feels like infrastructure for something serious — because that is what it is.

---

## How VERO Sounds (Voice and Tone)

### The Voice in One Sentence

Confident, calm, precise, and human. Like someone who knows what they are talking about and does not need to convince you with exclamation marks.

### Word Choices

**Use:**
- Direct, declarative sentences
- Specific numbers when meaningful ("250 verified workers in 90 days" — not "many users")
- Plain language ("we verify identity" — not "we leverage cryptographic attestation")
- Real examples and real names (with permission)

**Avoid completely:**
- "Just" ("just sign up" — banned)
- "Simply" ("simply browse jobs" — banned)
- "Easily" / "Effortlessly" (banned)
- "Revolutionize" / "Disrupt" (banned)
- "AI-powered" as a buzzword (only use if technically accurate and explained)
- "Solutions" as a vague catch-all
- "Game-changer" / "10x" / "Next-generation"
- Em dashes inside running copy (use a colon or a period instead)

### Example Comparisons

| Bad | Good |
|-----|------|
| "Easily build your professional brand with our AI-powered platform" | "Build a record of verified work that you actually own." |
| "Join thousands of happy users transforming their careers" | "Workers complete real jobs. Records are signed by both sides. Your work history belongs to you." |
| "Hire smarter with our revolutionary trust technology" | "See verified work history before you hire. Pay through escrow. Sign on completion." |

### Calls to Action

**Good:**
- "Join early access"
- "See how it works"
- "Build your profile"
- "Become a verified contributor"
- "Post your first job"

**Bad:**
- "Learn more"
- "Click here"
- "Submit"
- "Let's go"
- "Get started" (overused, vague)

## How VERO Looks (Visual Identity)

### Color

VERO uses a restrained, premium color system built on OKLCH color space (a modern way of defining colors that produces more consistent perceived brightness across hues).

**Dark mode (default):**
- Background: graphite — deep neutral, not pure black
- Surfaces (cards, panels): tinted graphite shades, slightly lighter
- Text: warm off-white for primary content
- Accent: a restrained champagne (warm metallic gold) — used sparingly
- Signal cyan: only for verification marks (signature, hash, escrow indicators)

**Light mode:**
- Background: warm off-white — never pure white
- Surfaces: subtly tinted whites
- Text: deep graphite
- Accent: deeper champagne
- Signal cyan: same purpose as in dark mode

**Color rules:**
- Never use pure black (#000) or pure white (#fff) anywhere
- The champagne accent appears on no more than 10% of any screen
- Verification cyan is reserved for trust-related marks only — not for general UI

### Typography

VERO uses three typefaces, each with a specific job:

1. **Geist Sans** — primary typeface, used for almost everything (headlines, body text, buttons, navigation). Modern, clean, highly legible.

2. **Geist Mono** — monospace typeface, used only for technical identifiers (hashes, IDs, signature glyphs, timestamps). Never used decoratively.

3. **Spectral** (italic only) — editorial accent typeface, used only for pull quotes — 1 or 2 per page maximum, never for headings or labels.

**Typography rules:**
- Headlines do not use italic
- All-caps tracked text above section headers is banned (looks dated and editorial-cliché)
- Drop caps are banned
- Body text line length stays between 62 and 72 characters for readability

### Layout and Spacing

- 8-point grid system — all spacing in multiples of 8 pixels
- Major rhythm uses 24, 48, 96, 144, and 192 pixel spacing between sections
- Section padding is intentionally varied, not uniform, to create cadence
- Generous whitespace — readable, never dense

### Motion and Animation

Motion is used to communicate, never to decorate.

- **Subtle reveals** when a page or section loads
- **Smooth scrolling** behavior (via Lenis)
- **Magnetic buttons** with a small snap-to behavior on hover
- **Trust system reveals** — when a verified record appears, the verification mark animates in with intent

**Motion rules:**
- Never animate layout properties (width, height, top, left) — only transform and opacity
- Animation durations: 180ms for micro-interactions, 320ms for reveals, 600ms for major scene changes
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — users who have asked their device to minimise animation get a static experience

## What VERO Looks Like (Design Reference Points)

VERO should feel like:
- **Stripe** — for clarity and confidence
- **Linear** — for interaction quality and precision
- **Apple** — for visual restraint
- **Financial Times** — for editorial seriousness
- **Patagonia** — for understated trust

VERO must never feel like:
- Fiverr or Upwork — bidding-war aesthetic
- Generic SaaS landing pages — hero metric + three-up icon grid template
- Crypto / web3 projects — neon gradients, holographic visuals
- AI-slop futurism — purple gradients, glowing chips
- LinkedIn or Behance — vanity profile aesthetic
- Startup cliché — "Trusted by [logos]" strips, "10x your output" copy

---

## Banned Visual Elements

These will not appear in VERO under any circumstances:

- Gradient text effects
- Purple, neon, or holographic colors
- 3D floating glassy elements that do not serve a purpose
- "Trusted by [logo strip]" social proof bars
- Big-number metric strips ("8M users / 99.99% / 5x faster")
- Three-up icon-and-title card grids that look identical to every other SaaS site
- Floating chatbot popups
- Lottie animations as decoration

---

## Accessibility Standards

VERO meets WCAG AA accessibility standards across the board:

- All interactive elements are reachable by keyboard
- Focus rings are visible and clear
- Color contrast meets WCAG AA on body content, AAA where possible
- Semantic HTML (proper headings, landmarks, ARIA labels)
- Reduced motion is fully supported
- Screen readers can navigate the entire product

Accessibility is not a checklist at the end. It is part of how VERO is built from the start.

## The Brand Promise

VERO helps people move from uncertainty to verified capability. It exists to make work visible, trusted, and earned.

Everything VERO publishes — every page, every email, every social post, every product screen — should reinforce that promise. If a piece of content does not contribute to it, the content should be rewritten or removed.
