# What We Are Building First (MVP)

This document describes the first version of VERO — what it will do when it launches, what it will deliberately not do yet, and why those choices were made.

---

## The One-Line Goal for Phase 1

Enable workers and businesses in Bengaluru to complete real, verified, dual-signed work transactions — and build a permanent, tamper-resistant record of each one.

---

## What Is In (Phase 1)

### For Workers

**Identity and verification**
- Sign up with phone number and email
- Verify identity (phone OTP, optional government ID for higher trust tier)
- Set career path (from 14 supported categories)

**Profile**
- Skills and specialisation declaration
- Portfolio uploads (images, links, documents)
- Public work record page showing all completed signed jobs

**Finding work**
- Browse available job listings by category and city area
- Single-screen application (no bidding, no price negotiation required at apply stage)
- View job scope and agreed payment before applying

**Executing work**
- View active job with scope, timeline, and escrow status
- In-job messaging with the client
- Submit deliverable and trigger completion request

**Getting paid and recorded**
- Dual-signature flow — both sides confirm completion
- Payment released from escrow on confirmation
- Permanent signed record added to public work profile
- Dispute path available if something goes wrong

### For Businesses

**Identity and verification**
- Sign up with business name, phone, email
- Optional: GST number for verified business tier

**Posting work**
- Create a job: category, scope, budget, city zone, timeline
- Set pay type: fixed price or day rate

**Reviewing and hiring**
- See applicant work records — signed history, trust standing, past client ratings
- Choose who to hire (no blind matching in Phase 1 — businesses choose directly)
- Fund escrow via Razorpay before work begins

**Completing and paying**
- Confirm work is done, provide second signature
- Payment releases to worker automatically
- Record of the hire saved to business dashboard

**Dashboard**
- View all active and completed hires
- See payment history
- Track workers you have hired before

### Shared Features

**Dispute system** — three tiers: direct, mediated, formally reviewed
**In-job messaging** — real-time thread per job
**Email notifications** — for key events: application received, hired, payment held, completion requested, dispute raised
**Admin panel** (internal, not public) — for VROE Labs team to manage verification queue, disputes, and user moderation

---

## What Is Deliberately Not In Phase 1

These features are excluded from the first version — not because they are unimportant, but because they would slow down the launch and distract from proving the core mechanics work.

| Feature | Why excluded |
|---------|-------------|
| AI-powered job matching | Phase 1 is about proving the record system works. Matching comes when there is enough data. |
| Blockchain-anchored credentials | Phase 5. Requires the trust graph to be mature first. |
| Mobile app (iOS / Android) | Phase 2. Web app is mobile-responsive. Native apps come after PMF is proven. |
| Enterprise API | Phase 3. |
| Public leaderboards or city rankings | Phase 2 — only after enough density to make rankings meaningful. |
| Social feed or following system | Not on the roadmap. VERO is not a social network. |
| Bidding or price negotiation | Never. VERO does not want a race to the bottom. |
| Anonymous accounts | Never. All participants must be verified. |
| Subscription for workers | Never. Workers are always free. |

## What Success Looks Like at Phase 1 End

- Workers have completed and signed real jobs (target: 200+ signed records in 90 days)
- Businesses have made real hires and had escrow payments released (target: 50+ businesses)
- Dispute rate is below 10%
- At least 25% of businesses have made a repeat hire
- Workers can see their public record and share it outside VERO
- Product is stable, responsive on mobile, and working without major bugs
