# VERO Privacy and Compliance Summary

This document explains, in plain language, how VERO handles user data, what laws it follows, and what rights users have. Anyone — technical or not — should be able to read this and understand exactly how their data is treated.

---

## The Short Version

- VERO collects only what is needed to make the platform work
- Your data is encrypted and protected at every layer
- You own your data — you can export it, see it, and request deletion
- VERO complies with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023
- VERO does not sell your data to anyone, ever
- Sensitive data (ID documents, financial info) is treated with extra care

---

## What Data VERO Collects

### From All Users

- **Identity basics:** name, phone number, email address
- **Login credentials:** password (never stored in plain text — see below)
- **Verification status:** whether your identity is confirmed, and at what tier
- **Usage data:** which pages you visit, which buttons you click (anonymised, for product improvement)

### From Workers

- **Profile data:** skills, career path, portfolio uploads, public bio
- **Work history:** completed jobs, signed records, endorsements
- **Trust signals:** completion rate, punctuality, dispute history
- **Identity documents (optional, for higher trust tier):** government-issued ID

### From Businesses

- **Business information:** name, type, location, optional GST number
- **Hiring activity:** job posts, hires, payments, completions
- **Payment information:** bank or card details (processed through Razorpay, not stored by VERO directly)

### What VERO Does Not Collect

- Your contacts list
- Your location continuously (only at job-relevant moments)
- Your social media activity outside VERO
- Browsing data from sites other than VERO
- Data about you from third-party data brokers

## How Your Data Is Protected

### Encryption

- **In transit:** All data sent between your device and VERO's servers is encrypted using HTTPS (the same standard banks use)
- **At rest:** Sensitive data stored in VERO's database is encrypted
- **Files:** Portfolio uploads, ID documents, and other files are stored in encrypted storage

### Password Handling

Your password is never stored in plain text. It is hashed using Argon2id — the current best-practice standard for password protection.

**What this means:** Even if someone were to gain access to VERO's database, they would not be able to see anyone's actual password. Even VERO does not know your password.

### Access Control

- Row-level security in the database — your data can only be accessed by you and those you have authorised
- Internal admin access is logged and audited
- Sensitive operations require additional authentication

### Rate Limiting

- Login attempts are limited (5 attempts per 15 minutes) to prevent brute-force attacks
- Signup attempts are limited (10 per hour per IP) to prevent automated abuse

### CSRF and Input Protection

- Every state-changing action requires a one-time token
- All inputs are validated before being processed — no unsanitised data reaches the database

## DPDP Act 2023 Compliance (India's Data Protection Law)

VERO is built to meet the requirements of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, India's primary data protection law.

### What This Means in Practice

**Consent:** VERO asks for your consent before processing your personal data, and explains why. You can withdraw consent at any time.

**Purpose limitation:** VERO only uses your data for the purposes you have agreed to (running the platform, completing your jobs, processing payments, providing support).

**Data minimisation:** VERO collects only what is needed. If a feature does not require a piece of data, VERO does not collect it.

**Accuracy:** You can update your personal information at any time. If something is wrong, you can correct it.

**Storage limitation:** Personal data is retained only as long as needed for the platform to function, except where Indian law requires longer retention (e.g. audit logs, financial records).

**Right to erasure:** You can request deletion of your account. Your personal data is then removed within the legally permitted window (some data, like financial records, must be retained for tax and compliance reasons).

**Right to portability:** You can request a copy of your data, including your verified work record, in a portable format.

**Right to grievance redressal:** If you have a concern about how your data is handled, you have a clear way to raise it and a defined response time.

### Soft Deletes

When you delete your account, your data is not immediately removed from VERO's systems. Instead:

- Your profile and personal data become inaccessible publicly and internally
- Your account is marked as deleted in the database
- Audit logs (required by Indian law for at least 7 years) are retained but anonymised where possible
- After the legal retention window, your data is permanently deleted

This approach is required by Indian law (which mandates audit log retention for certain activities) and is also a safety measure (it prevents accidental data loss and supports compliance reviews).

### Audit Logs

VERO keeps logs of important events: logins, payments, signatures, disputes, account changes. These logs:

- Are retained for at least 7 years (per Indian compliance requirements)
- Cannot be edited after they are written
- Are used only for security, fraud prevention, compliance reviews, and dispute resolution
- Are not used for marketing or advertising

## What VERO Will Never Do

- **Sell your data** to advertisers, brokers, or any third party
- **Share your data** with anyone outside VERO except where legally required (e.g. court order, compliance audit)
- **Track you across the web** outside the VERO platform
- **Use your private data** for marketing without explicit consent
- **Hide what we are doing** with your data — full disclosure in this document and the privacy policy

## Sharing Data with Third Parties

VERO uses a small number of carefully chosen third-party services to operate the platform. Each one has access only to what is necessary.

| Third party | What they see | Why |
|------------|--------------|-----|
| Supabase | Encrypted database and storage | Provides the platform infrastructure |
| Vercel | Web traffic | Hosts the website |
| Razorpay | Payment transactions, name, contact | Processes payments and escrow (RBI-licensed in India) |
| Sentry | Error reports (anonymised where possible) | Identifies and helps fix bugs |
| PostHog | Anonymised usage data | Product analytics — what features are used, where users get stuck |
| Resend (or equivalent) | Email address, message contents | Sends transactional emails (verification, notifications) |

Each of these providers is GDPR/DPDP-compliant. None of them sell user data.

## Sensitive Data — Extra Care

Some data is treated with additional protection:

- **Government-issued ID documents:** stored in encrypted private storage, accessible only by VERO's verification team during identity review. Not visible publicly. Not shared with any third party.
- **Payment information:** card and bank details are tokenised by Razorpay — VERO never sees or stores raw payment data.
- **Dispute records:** evidence and communications in disputes are accessible only to involved parties and VERO's mediation team. Dispute outcomes are noted on trust scores in summary form (without revealing personal details).

## What Happens If There Is a Data Breach

VERO has a defined incident response plan:

1. **Detection** — security monitoring is in place to detect unusual activity
2. **Containment** — affected systems are isolated to prevent further exposure
3. **Investigation** — what happened, what data was affected, who is impacted
4. **Notification** — users whose data may have been exposed are notified within the legally required window (typically 72 hours under DPDP Act and GDPR-aligned best practices)
5. **Remediation** — fix the underlying issue, harden against repeat events
6. **Disclosure** — public summary of the incident, what happened, and what users should do

VERO commits to transparency in the event of an incident. Hiding breaches damages trust permanently.
