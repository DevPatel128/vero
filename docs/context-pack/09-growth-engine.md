# Growth Engine

## Objective
Create growth systems that generate real traction without turning the product into addictive entertainment. The goal is to create trust-based virality, local adoption, and reputation-driven sharing.

## Growth philosophy
Growth should come from proof, progress, and social credibility. Users should want to share because their work identity improves them. The product should reward real contribution, not vanity.

## Core growth loops
1. user completes work
2. user earns proof and trust
3. user shares progress or profile
4. other users see the credibility
5. new users join to build the same value
6. local density improves
7. more opportunities appear
8. the network becomes more valuable

## High-value growth features
- public proof-of-work profiles
- shareable achievement cards
- city reputation pages
- career reputation levels
- apprenticeship streaks
- referral reputation
- campus ambassador program
- “Verified by VERO” badges
- real journey stories
- early access referrals
- partner recognition

## Shareable assets
Shareables should look polished and useful:
- first verified work
- trust milestone
- apprenticeship completion
- top-rated week
- verified badge
- career ladder unlock
- city ranking or community recognition

These assets should encourage pride and sharing without feeling like social media bait.

## Campus ambassador program
This can be a strong early growth lever. Ambassadors should help with:
- awareness
- onboarding
- local trust
- community education
- city activation
- campus-specific word of mouth

Ambassadors themselves should have credible status and tracked contribution.

## Referral design
Referrals should reward quality, not quantity alone. The system should track:
- referral conversion
- retention of referred users
- trust of referred users
- ambassador reliability

Avoid spam incentive designs. Weak referral systems become low-trust systems.

## City-level networks
Local density matters. Build reputational layers by city and neighborhood so users see the product as practical and local. Examples:
- top culinary contributors in Bengaluru
- trusted hospitality assistants in HSR
- verified creatives in Whitefield

## Employer and creator partnerships
Partnerships should reinforce legitimacy. Businesses and creators can verify work, contribute to the ecosystem, and provide endorsements or repeat opportunities.

## Retention logic
Users stay when they see:
- visible progress
- meaningful reputation
- repeat opportunities
- better matches
- trust growth
- practical value

## Safety on growth
Never let growth mechanics become manipulative. The system should encourage healthy progress, not addiction. Avoid endless scrolling, excessive notifications, and gimmick rewards.

## Product outcome
The growth engine should make VERO feel like a living trust network where real work is seen and valued.
