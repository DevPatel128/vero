# 09 — Future Scopes

## Purpose
Document where the company is going next while keeping the roadmap grounded in current product truth.
This file is the source of truth for future direction; the website page mirrors it for public reading.

## Research reserve
- Reserve 25% of the operating budget for research, validation, customer discovery, competitive scanning, and AI-assisted context maintenance.
- Treat the reserve as a standing operating rule, not an optional extra.
- Use the research budget to keep the product story, website, and AI guidance current.

## Audience alignment
- Investors: see disciplined growth and intentional use of capital.
- Users: see steady improvement without losing clarity or trust.
- Companies: see a platform with room for enterprise needs and adoption.
- AI agents: know where future scope is documented before they extend the pack.

## What stays fixed
- Theme lock
- Pricing guardrails
- Compliance posture
- Shared trust and crypto rules
- Pack structure and change control

## What can evolve
- Product scope
- Enterprise features
- Region-specific pages and policies
- AI guidance and research notes
- Launch sequencing and market emphasis

## Rule
Future scope content must remain grounded in current business priorities, compliance boundaries, and product reality.
