# 00 — VROE Labs Overview

## Purpose
VROE Labs builds a family of proof-based products that share trust infrastructure, shared tooling, and a common documentation standard.

## Products
- Vero — proof-of-work identity
- RIE — proof-of-discipline
- Trove — proof-of-provenance

## Audience
- Investors who need the business and moat story quickly
- Non-technical readers who need the plain-language version
- Developers who need the implementation constraints
- AI agents that need context, not guesswork

## What this file must answer
- What the company is
- What each product does
- Why the products belong together
- What is shared versus product-specific
- Where launch, pricing, and revenue live

## Anti-patterns
- Treating the company like a generic startup shell
- Hiding product differences behind vague umbrella language
- Forcing readers to jump across too many files before they understand the system
