# 06 — Revenue Models

## Scope
Document how each product makes money now and how it can make money later.

## Revenue types to cover
- Subscription / SaaS
- Transaction or escrow fees
- Enterprise plans
- Add-ons and power features
- Institutional or custom pricing
- Future monetization paths if they are credible

## What must be explicit
- What is free
- What is paid
- Who pays
- Why they pay
- Which model fits each product
- Which revenue path is primary versus experimental

## Anti-patterns
- Revenue hidden behind product prose
- Monetization ideas without a product-owner rationale
- Assuming enterprise pricing without enterprise value
- Calling everything freemium without specificity
