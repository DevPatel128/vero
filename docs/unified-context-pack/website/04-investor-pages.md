# 04 — Investor Pages

## Goal
Define how the company presents itself to investors.

## Must cover
- Market
- Problem
- Product family
- Pricing and revenue
- Moat and trust system
- Launch and growth posture
- Future scopes and research reserve

## Rule
Investor pages should be concise, credible, and grounded in the actual product system.

## Landing rule
The investor landing page should answer what the company is, why it matters, how it makes money, and why the research budget is intentional.
