# 09 — User Landing

## Goal
Explain the products in plain language so users immediately understand what they do and why they matter.

## Must cover
- What Vero does
- What RIE does
- What Trove does
- Why the shared trust layer matters
- How privacy and legal pages protect users
- What changes and what stays stable over time

## Tone
- Clear
- Calm
- Helpful
- Human

## Core promise
This page should make the products feel useful, trustworthy, and easy to approach.

## Rule
Avoid jargon unless it is immediately explained in plain language.
