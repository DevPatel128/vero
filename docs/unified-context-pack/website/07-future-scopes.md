# 07 — Future Scopes

## Purpose
Explain where the company is going next without drifting into vague speculation.
This page should help investors, users, companies, and AI agents understand the roadmap, the research budget, and what is intentionally left stable.

## Research reserve
- Reserve 25% of the product budget for research, validation, customer discovery, competitive scanning, and AI-assisted context maintenance.
- Treat that reserve as a strategic operating rule, not an optional leftover.
- Use the research work to keep the website, product docs, and agent guidance current.

## Audience alignment
- Investors should see discipline, not random expansion.
- Users should see that improvement is intentional and stable.
- Companies should see a platform that can grow into enterprise needs.
- AI agents should know where future scope is documented before they edit or extend the pack.

## What stays fixed
- Theme lock
- Pricing guardrails
- Compliance posture
- Shared trust and crypto rules
- The pack structure itself

## What can evolve
- Product scope
- Enterprise features
- Region-specific pages and policies
- AI guidance and research notes
- Launch sequencing and market emphasis

## Rule
Future scopes must remain grounded in product truth, current business priorities, and current compliance boundaries.
