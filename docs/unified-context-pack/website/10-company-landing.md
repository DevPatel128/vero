# 10 — Company Landing

## Goal
Speak to companies, procurement teams, and enterprise buyers who need confidence before they evaluate or adopt the products.

## Must cover
- Enterprise fit
- Security and compliance posture
- Pricing clarity
- Operating model and change control
- Rollout readiness
- Future scopes and roadmap discipline

## Tone
- Professional
- Direct
- Procurement-friendly
- Structured

## Core promise
This page should show that the products are ready for real company use, not just concept work.

## Rule
Make the business case obvious without overselling or hiding the operational details.
