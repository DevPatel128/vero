# 08 — Investor Landing

## Goal
Give investors a fast, credible read on the company and why it is worth paying attention to.

## Must cover
- Company thesis
- Product family overview
- Market and problem framing
- Pricing and revenue model
- Trust, compliance, and governance
- Future scopes and research reserve

## Tone
- Confident
- Specific
- Measured
- Evidence-led

## Core promise
This page should show that the company is disciplined, understandable, and designed to scale without losing trust.

## Rule
Investors should be able to understand the business in under a minute, then drill deeper without hitting vague language.
