# 05 — Country Pricing

## Goal
Adapt Trove pricing to local market conditions without weakening the trust-first positioning.

## Inputs
- Purchasing power
- Local storage, security, and trust-market benchmarks
- Tax and invoicing requirements
- Currency and payment rail friction
- Institutional demand by region

## Operating rules
- Keep a meaningful free tier
- Localize pro pricing by region when needed
- Maintain clear floors and ceilings
- Document exceptions and review cadence

## Anti-patterns
- FX-only pricing
- Treating every region as a premium market
- Undocumented institutional pricing
