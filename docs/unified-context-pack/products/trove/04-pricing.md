# 04 — Pricing

## Current pricing posture
- Freemium core
- Pro tier for deeper usage
- Institutional or export-based pricing where justified

## Pricing logic
The free experience should help users trust the system. Paid tiers should unlock power, scale, and institutional needs.

## Guardrails
- Protect the usefulness of the free tier
- Keep pricing tied to value and trust
- Do not overcomplicate the entry model
- Keep public pricing aligned with actual product limits

## Anti-patterns
- Hiding the real value behind paywalls
- Pricing that feels punitive for normal users
- Confusing consumer and institutional tiers
