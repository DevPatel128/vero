# 00 — Overview

## What Trove is
Trove is a proof-of-provenance product for value, credentials, and records people want to keep and trust over time.

## Who it is for
- People organizing important credentials
- Families and individuals tracking value and records
- Users who need durable proof and exportable history
- Institutions that need trust in stored records

## Why it exists
Trove makes important information durable, portable, and easier to verify.

## Core promise
What matters should stay trustworthy.

## Anti-patterns
- Treating Trove like a generic storage app
- Overexposing private value records
- Making provenance feel abstract instead of useful
