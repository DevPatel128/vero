# 24 — Compliance

## Goal
Capture the legal and compliance posture for Trove.

## Must cover
- Privacy and data handling
- Regional legal pages
- Sensitive information handling
- Data export and deletion
- Cross-border readiness where relevant

## Compliance rule
Public claims must match the actual privacy and security controls.

## Anti-patterns
- Vague legal language
- Compliance treated as decoration
- Claims that overstate the product's protections
