# 13 — Performance

## Goal
Keep the product quick and dependable even when handling sensitive records.

## Must cover
- Startup speed
- Navigation speed
- Sync or refresh expectations
- Asset discipline
- Caching strategy
- Monitoring and alerting

## Performance rule
Trust degrades when the product feels slow or fragile.

## Anti-patterns
- Slow record browsing
- Heavy screens without need
- Performance goals without owners
