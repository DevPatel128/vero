# 05 — Country Pricing

## Goal
Adjust Vero pricing by country or region without losing margin control or user trust.

## First anchor
India is the first anchor market.

## Pricing inputs
- Purchasing power
- Local competitor pricing
- Exchange rate
- Tax obligations
- Payment friction
- Willingness to pay
- Product value in that market

## Operating rules
- Use a hybrid formula instead of FX alone
- Define floors and ceilings
- Record exceptions explicitly
- Review prices when local conditions change

## What the pack should answer
- What is the base price?
- What is the localized price?
- Why does the country price differ?
- What is the margin floor?
- What happens when the market is too small or too expensive?

## Anti-patterns
- One price for every market by default
- FX-only conversion
- Pricing that ignores local taxes or payment behavior
- Unjustified special cases
