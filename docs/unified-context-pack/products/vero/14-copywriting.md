# 14 — Copywriting

## Goal
Keep written language consistent, trustworthy, and easy to understand.

## Voice rules
- Short and plain
- Specific over clever
- Honest over promotional
- Calm over urgent

## Copy must support
- Product explanation
- Pricing clarity
- Trust and safety
- Error handling
- Launch and invite messaging

## Anti-patterns
- Hype language
- Empty startup buzzwords
- Overly poetic lines that hide meaning
