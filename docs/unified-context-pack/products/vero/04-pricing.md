# 04 — Pricing

## Current pricing posture
- Workers: free
- Businesses: SaaS subscription
- Escrow on paid work: 5%

## Pricing logic
Vero monetizes the side that benefits most from trust and verification: businesses hiring with confidence. Workers should not pay to build records.

## What must be visible
- Who pays
- What they get
- Why the fee exists
- What is free
- What is included in escrow cost

## Guardrails
- Prices need margin discipline
- Discounting must be controlled
- Local pricing must follow the country-pricing rules
- Pricing copy must stay aligned with the actual billing model

## Anti-patterns
- Hidden fees
- Ambiguous subscription promises
- Pricing that undercuts trust
- Copy that makes the product sound cheaper or more expensive than it is
