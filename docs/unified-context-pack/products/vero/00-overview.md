# 00 — Overview

## What Vero is
Vero is a verified proof-of-work identity platform. It helps people turn real jobs, apprenticeships, and completed work into portable credibility.

## Who it is for
- Students and beginners who need experience
- Career switchers who need proof of capability
- Skilled workers without strong portfolios
- Households, cafés, studios, and SMBs that need to hire with confidence

## Why it exists
Most products show claims or resumes. Vero shows verified work. The product exists to make experience visible, trustworthy, and portable.

## Launch reality
- First market: Bengaluru
- Early zones: Whitefield, HSR Layout, Koramangala, Sarjapur, Electronic City
- Launch posture: local, trust-driven, and deliberately narrow before expansion

## Core promise
Real work should create real credibility.

## Anti-patterns
- Treating Vero like a generic gig app
- Making the product feel global before it feels local
- Hiding the trust system behind marketing language
