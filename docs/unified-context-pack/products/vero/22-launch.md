# 22 — Launch

## Goal
Describe the launch posture and first release story.

## Launch content
- First market
- First zones
- Early categories
- Waitlist posture
- Launch communication rules

## Launch rule
Launch should feel focused, real, and local before it feels broad.

## Anti-patterns
- Global-sounding launch language
- A launch narrative without geographic focus
- Promising more categories than can be supported
