# 15 — Roadmap

## Goal
Describe the staged path from launch to maturity.

## Roadmap phases
- Pre-launch website and waitlist
- Bengaluru pilot
- Category expansion
- Trust and reputation maturity
- Wider city expansion
- Ecosystem growth

## Rule
Each new phase should preserve trust and clarity before scale.

## Anti-patterns
- Overexpanding too early
- Roadmap claims that outpace product readiness
- Feature lists that ignore operational capacity
