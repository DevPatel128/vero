# 09 — Growth

## Goal
Describe how Vero acquires users, activates them, and earns repeat use without diluting trust.

## Growth model
- Local trust-led acquisition
- Waitlist and invite motion
- Word of mouth through verified work
- SEO around verified work and local hiring

## What should be covered
- North star metric
- Activation path
- Retention loop
- Referral logic
- SEO and content opportunities
- Regional expansion logic

## Anti-patterns
- Growth at the cost of trust
- Viral loops that create fake behavior
- SEO that overpromises the product
