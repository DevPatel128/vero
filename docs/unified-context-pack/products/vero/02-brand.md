# 02 — Brand

## Brand direction
Vero should feel calm, premium, local, and credible. It is not loud, playful, or gimmicky.

## Voice
- Plain
- Honest
- Specific
- Respectful
- Confident without hype

## Messaging order
1. What Vero is
2. Why it matters
3. Why it is different
4. How it works
5. Why it is safe
6. How to join

## Visual feeling
- Clean typography
- Generous spacing
- Quiet confidence
- Trust-first layout choices
- Minimal but polished motion

## What the brand should not feel like
- Entertainment
- Clickbait
- Noise
- Crypto jargon
- Generic startup swagger

## Anti-patterns
- Over-selling before the user understands the product
- Mixing too many tones in one experience
- Letting marketing drift away from product truth
