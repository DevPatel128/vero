# 06 — Revenue

## Revenue model
Vero is built around three primary revenue paths.

## Primary paths
- Business SaaS subscriptions
- Escrow fees on paid work
- Higher-touch studio or enterprise plans

## Revenue questions this file must answer
- Who pays?
- For what value?
- How often?
- What scales with usage?
- What is recurring versus transactional?

## Business logic
The revenue model should support trust infrastructure, verification operations, and product growth without putting the cost burden on workers.

## Future paths
- Advanced analytics
- Repeat-hire tooling
- Premium business workflows
- Institutional or partner pricing where justified

## Anti-patterns
- Monetization that weakens adoption
- Revenue ideas without a clear buyer
- Treating every add-on like a core revenue stream
