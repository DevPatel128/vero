# 24 — Compliance

## Goal
Capture the legal and compliance posture for RIE.

## Must cover
- Privacy and data handling
- Wearable and biometric sensitivity where applicable
- Regional privacy pages
- Payments and taxes if monetized regionally
- Data access and deletion handling

## Compliance rule
Public claims must match what the system can truly verify.

## Anti-patterns
- Vague privacy language
- Compliance treated as an afterthought
- Claims that overstate what proof means
