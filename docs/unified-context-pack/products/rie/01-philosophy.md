# 01 — Philosophy

## Decision filter
RIE should reward discipline that is real, repeatable, and verifiable. The product should never drift into hype or shallow gamification.

## Principles
- Proof before pride
- Consistency over theatrics
- Honest measurement over inflated claims
- Private effort can still become portable proof
- User trust matters more than leaderboard noise

## What should be discouraged
- Fake streaks
- Empty motivational design
- Social-feed clutter
- Claims the system cannot validate

## What should be rewarded
- Repeatable discipline
- Verified sessions and outputs
- Honest progress tracking
- Clear recovery after missed days

## Anti-patterns
- Turning discipline into entertainment only
- Building around vanity rankings
- Making the product feel louder than it is useful
