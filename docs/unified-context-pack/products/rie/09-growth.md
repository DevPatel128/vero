# 09 — Growth

## Goal
Describe how RIE acquires and retains users without undermining discipline authenticity.

## Growth model
- Organic sharing of proof
- Category-specific SEO
- Community and challenge loops
- Creator and athlete recommendations

## Must cover
- North star metric
- Activation path
- Retention loop
- Referral mechanics
- Search and discoverability

## Anti-patterns
- Growth tricks that break trust
- Streak mechanics that encourage fraud
- Noise over substance
