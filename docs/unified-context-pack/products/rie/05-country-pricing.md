# 05 — Country Pricing

## Goal
Adapt RIE pricing to local markets while preserving the value of the free core and the paid power layer.

## Inputs
- Purchasing power
- Local creator and fitness market benchmarks
- App store and payment friction
- Tax and invoicing rules
- Regional demand for pro analytics and automation

## Operating rules
- Base the free core everywhere
- Localize paid tiers by market
- Keep a visible price floor
- Document exceptions and review dates

## Anti-patterns
- Local pricing that breaks the freemium promise
- Pricing only by exchange rate
- Treating all regions as identical
