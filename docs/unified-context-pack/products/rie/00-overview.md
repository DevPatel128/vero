# 00 — Overview

## What RIE is
RIE is a proof-of-discipline platform for fitness, content, and gaming. It turns consistent effort into verifiable proof.

## Who it is for
- Athletes and lifters
- Writers, creators, and makers
- Gamers and competitive players
- People who want their discipline to be measurable

## Why it exists
RIE is for people who want proof that effort happened, not just stories about effort.

## Core promise
Discipline should be visible, portable, and hard to fake.

## Anti-patterns
- Turning the product into hype
- Treating discipline like a social feed
- Adding claims the system cannot verify
