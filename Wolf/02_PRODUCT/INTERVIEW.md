# Founder Interview Protocol

## Method
Broad → specific. Ask neutral questions. Separate preference from evidence. Challenge contradictions without leading.

## Rounds
1. Idea
2. User/problem
3. Product
4. Experience
5. Business
6. Risk
7. Constraints
8. Success

## Stop condition
Stop when additional questions are unlikely to materially change the product, research plan, risk posture or decision.

## Output
Founder facts, preferences, constraints, unknowns, contradictions and decisions requiring research.
