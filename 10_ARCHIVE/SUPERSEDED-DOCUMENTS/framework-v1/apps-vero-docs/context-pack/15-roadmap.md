## Non-goals (we will not chase)
- Becoming a generic job board.
- Selling a candidate database.
- Issuing certifications.
- Building a social feed.
- Crypto token.

## Roadmap discipline
- Each milestone has a Definition of Done.
- Each milestone has a measurable success criterion (records minted, retention, etc.).
- Each milestone has a kill switch — a condition under which we pause and re-evaluate.

## Inputs to the roadmap
- North star metric movement.
- User research (worker + business interviews).
- Compliance shifts (DPDP rule changes, RBI / NPCI updates).
- Capacity (team size, oncall burden).
- Cross-product synergy with RIE + Trove.
