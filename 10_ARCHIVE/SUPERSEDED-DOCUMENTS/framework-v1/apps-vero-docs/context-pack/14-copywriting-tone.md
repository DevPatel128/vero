# 14 — Copywriting Tone

## Tone in one line
**Calm. Premium. Plain. Local. Honest.**

## Voice characteristics
- **Calm.** No urgency theater. No countdown timers.
- **Premium.** Sentences breathe. White space matters.
- **Plain.** A 14-year-old understands it.
- **Local.** Bengaluru by name. Whitefield, HSR, Koramangala, Sarjapur, Electronic City when it matters.
- **Honest.** Pre-launch is pre-launch.

## Style rules
- **One idea per sentence.** Break compound sentences.
- **Active voice.** _"Vero signs the record"_, not _"the record is signed by Vero"_.
- **Present tense** for product behavior.
- **Past tense** for accomplishments.
- **Future tense** with dates for roadmap.
- **Numerals** for numbers ≥ 10. Spelled-out for 1–9 unless data.
- **Currency** in INR by default. International equivalents in parentheses.
- **Dates** in human format with ISO in tooltips for records.

## Word lists
**Words we prefer**
- proof, trust, record, verified, signed, chain, local, real, careful, hand-built

**Words we never use** (unless context pack says yes)
- just, simply, easily, effortlessly, revolutionize, disrupt, AI-powered, unleash, hustle, gig economy, world-class, cutting-edge, seamlessly, robust, leverage, synergy

## Headlines
- ≤ 60 characters.
- Specific over abstract.
- Imperative is fine if natural.
- Punctuation in headlines is OK (period, question mark, em dash).

**Yes:** _"Proof of work, not posts about work."_
**No:** _"Revolutionize your career with AI-powered work matching!"_

## Microcopy patterns
- **CTAs:** verbs. _"Apply"_, _"Sign job"_, _"Mint record"_.
- **Confirmations:** quote the destructive thing. _"Delete this draft? You'll lose 3 unsaved fields."_
- **Errors:** name the problem, name the fix. _"Pin must be 6 digits. Try again."_
- **Empty states:** _what's missing_ + _what to do_. _"No verified jobs in Whitefield yet. Get notified when one is posted."_

## Email + push
- Subject line ≤ 50 characters.
- No emoji.
- One CTA per message.
- Footer includes the reason for the email and an unsubscribe link.
- Push notifications are reserved for time-sensitive events the user explicitly opted into.

## Long-form (blog, manifesto)
- Magazine register.
- 3–6 minute reads.
- Lead with a person, place, or moment.
- Photography supports the story.
- Avoid bullet-list-only structure.

## Trust language
- _"Verified"_ has a precise meaning — it means signed by at least one counterparty. Use it accurately.
- _"Proof"_ has a precise meaning — it means there is an ALVED record. Use it accurately.

## Legal-adjacent claims (never make without compliance review)
- Earnings promises.
- KYC speed promises.
- Visa / immigration relevance.
- Government endorsement claims.
- Health, safety, education outcome claims.

## Apprenticeship copy
- Always distinguish paid from unpaid.
- Always show duration cap.
- Never imply that unpaid apprenticeships are a substitute for paid work.

## Persona-specific tone
- **For workers:** practical, encouraging, plain.
- **For businesses:** confident, brief, results-focused.
- **For investors:** calm, numerate, honest.
- **For press:** factual, quotable.

## Anti-patterns
- "🚀 Launch your career with Vero! 💼" — banned.
- "Vero leverages cutting-edge AI to..." — banned.
- "Join thousands of happy Vero users today!" — banned pre-launch.

## When in doubt
Read the sentence aloud. If you would be embarrassed to read it to a thoughtful adult, rewrite it.
