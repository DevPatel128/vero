# 08 — Career Paths

## Why this matters
Vero is not a destination — it is a runway. Users need to see _what the next step is_ at every moment. Without that, they leave.

## The pipeline
```
Anonymous browser
    ↓
Phone-verified explorer
    ↓
First apprenticeship completed
    ↓
KYC-verified worker
    ↓
First paid record minted
    ↓
Trusted worker (multi-category)
    ↓
Master worker (badge, premium routing, mentor unlock)
```

## Categories at launch
Bengaluru-specific, picked for density + repeat-bookability:

- **Hospitality** — café shift, barista, kitchen prep, server, baker, host.
- **Home services** — house cleaning, plumbing assist, electrical assist, painting, AC service, gardening.
- **Care** — elder companion, child care, pet care, dog walking.
- **Skilled trade** — carpentry, welding, AC repair, bike mechanic, tailoring.
- **Mobility** — driver (rideshare-adjacent, school routes), parcel runner.
- **Content** — graphic design, social media support, video edits, content writing.
- **Tutoring** — school-level subjects, language classes, music basics.
- **Tech** — frontend, design, QA, basic data work.

Categories are expanded by demand, not by guess. Add a category only when ≥ 50 jobs / month exist in it locally.

## Badges + milestones
Badges are unlocked by verified records, never by paid promotion.

- **First Step** — 1 verified record.
- **Steady Hand** — 5 records in a category, on-time rate ≥ 85%.
- **Trusted Worker** — 15 records across ≥ 2 categories, dispute rate < 2%.
- **Master Worker** — 50 records, repeat-booking ratio ≥ 35%, dispute rate < 1%.
- **Mentor** — Master Worker + at least 3 vouched mentees.

Badges show on the public profile + appear in the JSON-LD.

## Apprenticeships
- Unpaid by definition.
- Capped at a duration per category (e.g. café apprenticeship max 30 days).
- Records are minted but marked `apprenticeship: true` in the record metadata.
- Anti-abuse: businesses cannot post more than a small ratio of apprenticeship vs paid work.

## Skill maps
- Each category has a skill ladder (e.g. _Barista_: latte basics → milk steaming → pour patterns → espresso calibration → bar lead).
- Skills are claimed by the worker but only confirmed when a verified record carries that skill tag and the counterparty signs.

## Pay tiers
- Driven by category × experience × city.
- Floor pay enforced — Vero refuses to post jobs below the local minimum wage.
- Pay tiers are visible on the for-businesses page so businesses know what fair looks like.

## Cross-product growth
- A worker who completes Vero records can opt into RIE — the discipline pattern compounds with the work pattern.
- A trusted worker on Vero gets early access to Trove (because they are exactly the household whose trades + skills generate value worth recording).

## Off-platform paths
- Vero can issue a **verifiable employment letter** as a PDF + JSON-LD. Useful for visa, loan, school applications. Pulled directly from records — no fabrication.

## What we don't do
- We don't sell certifications.
- We don't run paid training.
- We don't run sponsored matchmaking.
- We don't sell access to candidates.

These could be revenue someday. They are not Vero at launch.
