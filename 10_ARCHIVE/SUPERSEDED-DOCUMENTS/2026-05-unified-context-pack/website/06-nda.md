# 06 — Non-Disclosure Agreement

## Purpose
Provide a formal NDA surface for sensitive discussions, reviews, and partner conversations.

## What it should cover
- Confidential information definitions
- Permitted use
- Exclusions
- Term and survival
- Return or destruction of materials
- No public disclosure without permission

## Rule
This page should exist as part of the public website/legal system whenever the company needs a standard NDA reference.
