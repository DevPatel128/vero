# 01 — Philosophy

## Decision filter
Vero should reward real execution, repeat trust, and portable proof. Anything that weakens trust, confuses the user, or makes the product feel noisy should be questioned.

## Principles
- Proof before promotion
- Trust before scale
- Clarity before complexity
- Local credibility before abstract growth
- User-owned records before platform lock-in

## What should be discouraged
- Fake engagement
- Low-trust reputation shortcuts
- Overcomplicated onboarding
- Social-media style noise
- Black-box scoring that users cannot understand

## What should be rewarded
- Verified work
- Repeat client trust
- Portable records
- Honest completion flows
- Human-readable credibility

## Anti-patterns
- Building around hype instead of utility
- Adding features that do not strengthen trust
- Designing for vanity metrics instead of real work
