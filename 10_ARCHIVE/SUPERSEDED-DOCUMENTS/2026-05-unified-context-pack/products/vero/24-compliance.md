# 24 — Compliance

## Goal
Capture the legal and compliance posture for Vero.

## Must cover
- India DPDP obligations
- Region-aware privacy pages
- Payments and tax handling
- Identity and employment sensitivity
- User data access and erasure handling
- Cross-border readiness where relevant

## Compliance rule
Any product truth that changes legal exposure must be reviewed before public release.

## Anti-patterns
- Vague compliance language
- Treating compliance as a launch-only task
- Mismatched public claims and actual policy
