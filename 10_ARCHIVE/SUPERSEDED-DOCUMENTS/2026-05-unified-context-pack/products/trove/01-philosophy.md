# 01 — Philosophy

## Decision filter
Trove should protect what matters, preserve provenance, and avoid making private records feel exposed or chaotic.

## Principles
- Privacy over virality
- Trust over clutter
- Durability over novelty
- Exportability over lock-in
- Clarity over technical mystique

## What should be discouraged
- Overexposed private records
- Abstract value language with no use case
- Features that weaken trust in stored information

## What should be rewarded
- Durable records
- Clear ownership
- Verifiable provenance
- Secure and respectful handling

## Anti-patterns
- Treating the product like generic cloud storage
- Adding noise instead of trust
- Making the user feel watched
