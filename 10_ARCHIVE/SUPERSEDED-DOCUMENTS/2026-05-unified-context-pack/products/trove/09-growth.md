# 09 — Growth

## Goal
Describe how Trove grows by earning trust, not by chasing attention.

## Growth model
- Trust-led word of mouth
- Search visibility for record and provenance use cases
- Institutional credibility
- Careful referral patterns

## Must cover
- North star metric
- Activation path
- Retention loop
- Export and sharing behavior
- Search and discoverability

## Anti-patterns
- Viral tricks that harm trust
- Growth copy that sounds loud or generic
- Attention-first positioning
