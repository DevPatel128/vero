# 02 — Brand

## Brand direction
RIE should feel focused, calm, athletic, and honest. It should communicate discipline without hype.

## Voice
- Direct
- Quietly ambitious
- Specific
- Respectful
- Calmly motivating

## Messaging order
1. What RIE is
2. Why discipline matters
3. How proof is created
4. Why it is trustworthy
5. How to join

## Visual feeling
- Clean surfaces
- High signal layouts
- Athlete-grade clarity
- Minimal decorative motion
- Strong hierarchy

## Anti-patterns
- Shouty fitness marketing
- Buzzword-driven creator language
- Making the brand feel like a game first
