# 14 — Copywriting

## Goal
Keep language direct, disciplined, and honest.

## Voice rules
- Clear
- Calm
- Specific
- Motivating without hype

## Copy must support
- Discipline explanation
- Proof capture
- Category onboarding
- Error handling
- Pricing clarity

## Anti-patterns
- Gym-bro hype
- Noise-driven social copy
- Buzzwords that hide meaning
