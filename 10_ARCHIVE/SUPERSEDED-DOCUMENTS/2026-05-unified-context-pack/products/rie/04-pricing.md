# 04 — Pricing

## Current pricing posture
- Core product: free
- Pro features: paid
- Power features may include advanced charts, APIs, and premium analytics

## Pricing logic
The free layer should be enough to establish proof. Paid features should deepen insight, automation, and serious usage.

## Guardrails
- Keep the core experience accessible
- Tie paid features to real power use
- Avoid charging for basic proof capture
- Keep pricing copy aligned with the shipped feature set

## Anti-patterns
- Paywalling the core proof experience
- Confusing free versus paid capabilities
- Pricing that feels like a tax on discipline
