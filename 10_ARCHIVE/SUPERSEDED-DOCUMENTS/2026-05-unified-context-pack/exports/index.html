What investors should see

The company is a family of proof-based products with one trust layer, one documentation system, and one governance rulebook. Pricing, revenue, and rollout are explicit, which makes the business easier to understand.

What customers should feel

Each product makes a clear promise: Vero turns work into verified proof, RIE turns discipline into something visible, and Trove keeps important records trustworthy and portable.

What technologists should understand

The stack is documentation-first: shared rules, product packs, logs, HTML preview, and a controlled change policy. The pack exists to reduce drift, not to add complexity.

What companies should understand

The products are enterprise-readable, procurement-friendly, and built to align with team workflows, compliance needs, and long-term operating use.

When the raw idea changes, research first, then add the missing materials, then update logs and this single export file if the structure itself changes.
