## Brand attributes

Calm, premium, trustworthy, intelligent, practical, youth-oriented, human, modern, progressive, focused (archived `CLAUDE.md` §5).

## Naming

- **VROE Labs** — the company.
- **VERO** — the product.
- **ALVED** (Authentic Ledger of Validated Evolution Data) — the shared trust protocol.

Use these names consistently and precisely; inconsistent naming undermines the trust the product is selling.

## Voice, in one sentence

Confident, calm, precise, human. Like someone who knows what they are talking about and does not need exclamation marks to convince you (`Documents/11`).

## Word choices

**Use:** direct, declarative sentences; specific numbers when meaningful and real (e.g. "250 verified workers in 90 days" — never a placeholder passed off as real); plain language over jargon; real examples with permission.

**Never use** (banned outright, per `Documents/11` and the archived `CLAUDE.md` §5): *just, simply, easily, effortlessly, revolutionize, disrupt*, "AI-powered" as an unexplained buzzword, "solutions" as a vague catch-all, "game-changer," "10x," "next-generation." Em dashes are banned inside running copy — use a colon or period instead.

## CTA tone

**Good:** "Join early access," "See how it works," "Build your profile," "Become a verified contributor," "Post your first job."
**Bad:** "Learn more," "Click here," "Submit," "Let's go," "Get started" (overused, vague).

## Copy comparison examples (`Documents/11`)

| Bad | Good |
|---|---|
| "Easily build your professional brand with our AI-powered platform" | "Build a record of verified work that you actually own." |
| "Join thousands of happy users transforming their careers" | "Workers complete real jobs. Records are signed by both sides. Your work history belongs to you." |
| "Hire smarter with our revolutionary trust technology" | "See verified work history before you hire. Pay through escrow. Sign on completion." |

## Copy quality check

Before publishing any line: is it clear? Is it useful? Is it credible? Does it fit the brand? Does it help the user act? If not, revise.

## Claim discipline

Every product claim must map to something actually built or explicitly roadmapped (see `02_PRODUCT/PRODUCT.md`'s "Product lifecycle"), and every legal-sensitive claim (data, payments, employment, health, DPDP compliance) needs a source or explicit human/legal review before publishing — see `07_BUSINESS/LEGAL-COMPLIANCE.md` and the open SLA contradiction in `08_DECISIONS/PRODUCT/2026-09-open-contradictions.md`. Never invent a value, date, or statistic; write `UNKNOWN` or `TBD` and flag it rather than fabricate one (`01_PRINCIPLES/PRINCIPLES.md` rule 6).
