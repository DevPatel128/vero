# PRODUCT.md — VERO Freelance

## Register

`brand` — this is the pre-launch marketing surface. Design IS the product. Every section persuades, positions, and earns trust.

## Product Purpose

VERO Freelance is verified proof-of-work freelance and apprenticeship infrastructure. Two-sided trust marketplace where execution is the only credential that counts. Operators build a portable, signed work history. Clients hire from a pool whose record is already proof. The category is reputation infrastructure, not job board.

Primary narratives:
- LinkedIn shows claims. VERO shows proof.
- Talent is common. Verified execution is rare.
- Execution compounds.
- Work becomes identity.
- Proof matters more than presentation.

## Users

**Primary (18–28, high-agency):** ambitious students, creators, operators, developers, designers, editors, marketers, athletes, founders, young freelancers.

**Secondary:** startups, agencies, recruiters, local businesses, ecosystem partners.

**Decision moment on the site:** the operator must feel "I need to be early. This is the future of work. I should improve before applying." The business must feel "I can finally hire by proof, not presentation." The investor must feel "category-defining infrastructure, not a marketplace."

## Strategic Principles

1. Proof over claims. Show records, not testimonials.
2. Restrained over loud. Quiet confidence beats startup theatre.
3. 10–15% product reveal. Never expose full UI. Tease, do not document.
4. Globally scalable language. No Bengaluru framing on the marketing surface.
5. Earned tone. The site itself should feel like a credential to access.
6. Editorial seriousness. Financial Times confidence, not SaaS punch.

## Anti-references (must not feel like)

- Fiverr / Upwork (bidding war, dopamine UX)
- generic SaaS landing pages (hero-metric template, identical card grids)
- crypto / web3 hype (neon gradients, holographic visuals)
- AI-slop futurism (purple gradient, glowing chips, fake AI visuals)
- LinkedIn / Behance (vanity portfolio)
- startup cliché (3-up icon grids, hero promise of "10x your output")

## Reference companies (feels like)

Stripe (clarity), Linear (interaction), Apple (precision), Financial Times (editorial confidence), Patagonia (restraint), Notion (calm).

## Tone

Calm, premium, infrastructure-grade. Editorial cadence. Confident sentences. Short. No filler. Em dashes banned in copy.

## Voice rules

- Never: just / simply / easily / effortlessly / revolutionize / disrupt / AI-powered
- Never: vague urgency, fake counts, startup superlatives
- Yes: declarative statements. Numbers when meaningful. Pauses.
