## Engineering
Compared with DORA, Google SRE, AWS/Google Well-Architected, NIST SSDF, OWASP ASVS/SAMM, FinOps, Team Topologies and ADR practice.
V3 adds delivery metrics, SLI/SLO/error budgets, architecture pillars, secure-development lifecycle, application-security verification, security maturity, technology economics, team boundaries and historical decisions.

## Product
Compared with Continuous Discovery, Opportunity Solution Trees, Jobs To Be Done and empowered outcome-oriented product teams.
V3 adds continuous customer learning, opportunity mapping, job/situation framing, assumption testing and outcome-based autonomy.

## Design
Compared with Apple HIG, Nielsen heuristics, Double Diamond, accessibility practice and HEART.
V3 adds clarity, agency, feedback, recovery, measurement, predictive simplicity and progressive removal of repetitive complexity.

## Business
Compared with Business Model Canvas, Value Proposition Canvas, Strategy Choice Cascade, North Star metrics, experimentation and portfolio thinking.
V3 treats business models as hypotheses and makes strategic choices explicit.

## Research
V3 combines generative/evaluative, qualitative/quantitative, market, competitive, technical, security and AI evaluation into one evidence loop.

## Operations
V3 combines SRE, Well-Architected, FinOps and continuity into Build → Release → Observe → Recover → Learn → Change.

## AI governance
V3 incorporates NIST AI RMF's Govern/Map/Measure/Manage model plus least privilege, tool controls, prompt-injection defense, runtime monitoring, evaluation and auditability.

## Social/content
V3 benchmarks X, LinkedIn, Instagram/Meta, YouTube and Substack plus funnel, thought-leadership, creator/community, referral and experimentation systems.
