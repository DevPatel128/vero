# 04 — Pricing Governance

## Purpose
Define how pricing is decided, constrained, reviewed, and localized.

## Pricing rules
- Pricing must be justified by product value and market position.
- Every product gets explicit pricing documentation.
- Country pricing must use a hybrid formula.
- Floors and ceilings are required so margin does not collapse.
- Exceptions must be documented.

## Inputs to the pricing model
- Purchasing power
- Local competitor pricing
- Exchange rate
- Tax and regulatory constraints
- Product-specific willingness to pay
- Revenue goals

## Review policy
- Pricing changes must be reviewed against current product pages and launch plans.
- If pricing changes affect public copy, the context pack and marketing pages must be updated together where feasible.

## Anti-patterns
- One global price for every market without checking fit
- Discounting without guardrails
- Revenue assumptions hidden in product copy
- Prices that cannot be defended to investors or operators
