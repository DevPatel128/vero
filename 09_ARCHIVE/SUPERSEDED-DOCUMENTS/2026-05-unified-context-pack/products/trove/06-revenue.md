# 06 — Revenue

## Revenue model
Trove uses freemium plus premium tiers and selective institutional pricing.

## Revenue paths
- Pro subscriptions
- Institutional or team plans
- Export or workflow premium features
- Future value-added services where appropriate

## Revenue questions
- Who upgrades and why?
- Which records or workflows need premium handling?
- What should remain free to build trust?

## Anti-patterns
- Monetizing the core trust promise too early
- Hiding institutional pricing logic
- Turning provenance into a generic upsell machine
