# 15 — Roadmap

## Goal
Describe the staged evolution of Trove.

## Roadmap phases
- Core secure records
- Better provenance and export
- Privacy and sharing maturity
- Institutional use cases
- Ecosystem growth

## Rule
Each phase should strengthen trust before it broadens scope.

## Anti-patterns
- Expanding before the trust story is clear
- Roadmap items that weaken privacy
- Feature sprawl that outruns the core
