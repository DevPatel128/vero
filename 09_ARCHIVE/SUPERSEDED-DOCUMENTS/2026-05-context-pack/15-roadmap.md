# Roadmap

## Objective
Create a staged roadmap for VERO so the product grows in a controlled, trust-preserving way.

## Roadmap philosophy
The product should not expand randomly. It should deepen one layer before moving to the next. Every phase should preserve trust, clarity, and local market fit.

## Phase 1: Pre-launch website
Goals:
- explain VERO clearly
- build waitlist
- create buzz
- collect early users
- educate users before launch
- attract ambassadors and partners

## Phase 2: Bengaluru pilot
Goals:
- activate trust network in priority zones
- onboard early users and clients
- launch first career categories
- test verification and trust
- refine onboarding and matching
- build repeat usage
- handle disputes safely

## Phase 3: Category expansion
Add selected new categories only after the first ones are stable. Possible additions:
- elderly assistance
- pet care
- beauty and wellness
- hospitality
- tutoring
- home repair support
- creator economy support
- digital business support

## Phase 4: Trust and reputation maturity
Add better profile depth, verified milestones, stronger credibility systems, and more robust client reliability tracking.

## Phase 5: Blockchain extension
Add blockchain only when it clearly helps:
- portable credentials
- transparent escrow
- stablecoin rails
- trust portability
- verifiable certificates

## Phase 6: Wider city expansion
Expand after the trust model proves itself locally. Do not scale too early. Dense local trust is more valuable than shallow spread.

## Phase 7: Ecosystem growth
Potential future systems:
- mentor network
- employer network
- education partners
- city ambassador networks
- portable reputation integrations
- cross-platform verification

## Expansion rule
Do not add more categories, features, or cities than the team can verify and support well. Quality over expansion protects the brand.

## Product maturity markers
A phase is ready to advance when:
- trust is stable
- users understand the product
- clients are returning
- disputes are manageable
- category quality is strong
- the UX is clean
- performance is stable

## Roadmap outcome
The roadmap should help the team stay disciplined. VERO should grow like infrastructure, not like hype.
