Trust

How Vero is built to be hard to game.

Trust is a property of the substrate, not a slogan on the home page. The six principles below are what make a Vero record worth more than a screenshot.

Two signatures, one artefact

The smallest unit of trust on the platform.

01

Both sides sign

Every record carries two signatures. Neither side can sign alone. The platform never signs on either side's behalf.

02

Chained, not editable

Each record links by hash to the one before it for the same worker. Reordering, deleting, and rewriting are all detectable.

03

A graph, not a rating

Show-up rate, repeat-booking ratio, dispute history, counterparty quality, on-time rate, category strength — multiple signals, never one gameable number.

04

Escrow on paid work

The client funds the job up front. We hold it. Release is on the record. The worker can see the money is real.

05

Documented dispute path

Evidence collected. Small review team responds quickly. Outcome recorded on both sides. No silent resolutions.

06

Repeat-offender cut-off

Accounts proven to abuse the system lose access and are removed from the trust graph. Patterns are tracked across the network.

The shape of standing

A network of signed work. No stars.

Standing on Vero is not a number. It is a position in a graph — the clients you have worked with, the work they signed for, the patterns that emerge over time.

What we will never do

Promises with a sharp edge.

We will not silently edit a record. We will not change a worker's standing without an audit entry. We will not promote profiles for reasons the user cannot see. We will not sell your data, today or ever. We will not pre-tick consent boxes.

These are not features. They are constraints we accept in exchange for being allowed to call this a trust product.

Trust is the only thing worth carrying forward.
