01 / The problem

You have done good work. You just cannot prove it.

The hiring system in India is broken in a very specific way — not because people are dishonest, but because the tools we use make it impossible to tell who has actually done the work.

Why this matters now →
  • 01

    Resumes say whatever the person wants.

    Anyone can write anything on a resume. Most hiring managers know this — yet they have no better option. The result is that good work goes unseen and bad hires keep happening.

  • 02

    Gig platforms race to the bottom.

    Fiverr, Upwork, and their clones push everyone to bid lower and reply faster. The best person for the job rarely wins. The cheapest one does.

  • 03

    Portfolios can be faked in an afternoon.

    Screenshots can be copied. AI can generate a convincing case study in minutes. There is no way to know if the work was really done by the person showing it.

03 / How it works

Simple enough to explain in three steps.

The full flow — verification, escrow, signatures, dispute paths — is on the product page. Here is the short version.

See the full product →
  1. 01

    Verify once.

    Workers and businesses confirm their identity before they connect. No anonymous accounts.

  2. 02

    Do real work.

    Agree on scope, fund escrow if paid, complete the job. Nothing abstract — actual work, actual people.

  3. 03

    Both sides sign.

    When the job is done, both the worker and the client confirm it. The record is permanent and belongs to the worker.
