Features

The small details that make Vero serious.

Vero is engineered so the rules are the same for everyone, including us. The features below are the parts that make that claim mean something.

01 · Records

Signed, chained, and portable. The substance of the platform.

Two-party signing

Worker and client both sign. Neither can sign alone. The platform never signs for either.

Chained history

Each record references the one before it for the same worker. Reordering is detectable.

Visibility flags

Public, shared, or private. Workers choose per record. Privacy is the default for sensitive categories.

Portable export

Every record is exportable in a portable, signed format. The credentials you build can be read by other products that speak the same protocol.

02 · Standing

A trust graph, not a star rating. Many real signals, not one gameable number.

Completion reliability

Show-up rate, on-time rate, dispute rate, and repeat-booking ratio. Each is visible and explainable.

Counterparty quality

Working with verified, well-rated clients lifts your standing. Working with fraudulent accounts cannot.

Category strength

Standing is held per category. A kitchen assistant builds kitchen credibility — not generic stars.

Transparent reasons

Any change to your standing is explained on your dashboard. No black boxes.

03 · Money + safety

Escrow you can see. Disputes with a clear path. Coverage where it matters.

Funded escrow on paid work

The client funds the job up front. We hold it. The worker can see the money is real before showing up.

Flat 5% fee

Well below the market. We do not take a hidden cut, and we do not change the rate by category or city.

Dispute path

If something goes wrong, evidence is collected, a small review team responds quickly, and resolutions are recorded.

Repeat-offender cut-off

Accounts proven to abuse the system are cut off and removed from the trust graph. The pattern is detectable.

04 · Verification

Who you say you are, confirmed before paid work begins.

Phone verification

Required for every account. Quick. No new password to remember.

ID verification for paid work

Workers verify their ID before earning. We use government-supported digital documents — quick to do, hard to fake.

Business verification

Businesses verify their entity. We display the verification mark on profiles and on every record they sign.

No biometric data stored

We do not retain biometric data. Verification results are stored, the underlying scans are not.
