A record of real work, owned by you.

Vero is free for workers. Always. We do not charge you to create your record, to keep it, or to take it elsewhere.

The hard parts

Three loops that work against beginners.

You need experience to get hired

And you need to get hired to get experience. Most platforms reward the people who already have records.

Your past does not travel

What you build on one app stays trapped there. The next platform makes you start from zero.

Your word counts for nothing

Without a verified history, claiming what you can do does not move anyone. Claims are everywhere.

Verified, not vouched

An identity anyone can verify.

Your phone, your ID, and your signed history form a single artefact. Anyone with the public key can check it. The platform is not a privileged party.

What changes on Vero

Small work. Signed. Yours.

Begin small, signed, and real

Apprenticeships and short gigs that fit your life today. Each one signed by you and the person who hired you.

Carry your record forward

Every record is exportable. The credentials you build on Vero can be read by other platforms that speak the same protocol.

Your standing is explained

Show-up rate. Repeat clients. Dispute history. No black-box number. You can see exactly why you are where you are.

Privacy is yours to choose

Each record carries a visibility flag — public, shared, or private. Private records still count toward your standing.

Stacks over time

One signed record, then another, then another.

Each record references the one before it for you. The chain is yours to carry into the next opportunity, on Vero or elsewhere.

Who Vero is for

Different people. Same need.

  • Students

    Before the first résumé line lands, build a small record of paid and unpaid work. It will outweigh a part-time at a coffee chain.

  • Career switchers

    Begin in the new direction with verified, lower-stake work. Stack completions until the next employer cannot ignore you.

  • Skilled hands

    Carpenters, electricians, cooks, beauticians, stylists, repair technicians. People who do real work and have nothing online to show for it.

  • Creators + freelancers

    Designers, editors, writers, developers. Stop relying on screenshots and unverifiable Behance links. Sign the work.
