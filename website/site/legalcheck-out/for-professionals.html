For professionals

You have done the work. Now you can prove it.

Vero is free for workers. Always. Build a verified record of completed jobs — signed by the people who hired you — that you own and carry wherever you go.

Join as a professionalHow it works

The hard parts

Three loops that work against talented people.

  • You need experience to get hired, but you need to be hired to get experience.

    Most platforms only reward people who already have records. Starting out — or starting over — is disproportionately hard.

  • Your work history disappears when you leave a platform.

    Three years of great jobs on one app count for nothing on the next one. You start from zero every single time.

  • Saying you did good work is not the same as proving it.

    Without a verified record, the only thing you have is your word. And when everyone else has screenshots and LinkedIn posts, your word is not enough.

What changes on Vero

Your work speaks for itself. Literally.

Every job you complete becomes a signed entry in your record. The person who hired you signs it too. That record is permanent, portable, and belongs to you — not the platform.

  • Start small. Build real.

    Short gigs, one-off jobs, apprenticeships — everything counts. Each completed job, no matter how small, becomes a signed entry in your record.

  • Your record travels with you.

    Your Vero record is portable. It is not locked to this platform. When the next platform comes along, your history comes with you.

  • You can see exactly where you stand.

    Show-up rate, repeat clients, dispute history — all visible, all explained. No black-box score that shifts for no reason.

  • Privacy is yours to decide.

    Each record can be public, shared only with specific people, or private. Private records still count toward your standing.

Who Vero is for

Different work. Same problem.

The credential gap hits every kind of worker. Vero is built for all of them.

  • Students

    Build a record before your first resume line.

    Start with small paid or unpaid work before you graduate. A signed record of real jobs will outweigh an unverified LinkedIn entry every time.

  • Career switchers

    Prove your new direction without a long wait.

    Take verified work in the field you are moving into. Stack completions. By the time you apply for a full-time role, you have a track record, not just a plan.

  • Skilled trades

    Carpenters, electricians, cooks, beauticians — your work deserves proof too.

    Millions of people do excellent hands-on work and have nothing to show for it online. Vero treats every job as a real entry in a real record.

  • Freelancers

    Stop relying on screenshots and unverifiable portfolios.

    Designers, developers, writers, editors — sign the work. A signed record from a real client is worth more than a Behance link anyone could have made.

How records stack

One job. Then another. Then another.

Each record is linked to the one before it. That continuity turns separate jobs into a career history that anyone can read and no one can fake.

What we promise workers

Four commitments. No asterisks.

  • Vero is free for workers. Always. We will never charge you to create, keep, or export your record.
  • No record will ever be silently edited, downgraded, or removed by the platform.
  • You can export your full record in a portable format at any time.
  • If we cannot keep these promises, we will say so before we break them.

Your record starts the first time you sign.
