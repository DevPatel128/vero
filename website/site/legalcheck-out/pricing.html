Pricing FAQ

What people ask before joining.

Do workers pay anything to use Vero?
No. We do not charge workers. Not a subscription, not a per-record fee, not a withdrawal fee. The platform is free to use, and your record is free to keep, forever.
What is the 5% escrow fee for?
It funds escrow infrastructure, dispute operations, identity verification, and basic fraud cover. It is flat rather than category-dependent.
What about taxes?
Prices shown are pre-tax. GST applies for Indian customers. Subscription invoices, escrow receipts, and tax-compliant statements are issued automatically.
Can I cancel a business plan any time?
Yes. Monthly plans cancel monthly. Your verified records and signed history remain available according to the product terms.
What if I post a role and nobody takes it?
There is no failure cost. The role expires. Your subscription is for access and verification, not for guaranteed hires.
