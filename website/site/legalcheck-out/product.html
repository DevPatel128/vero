How Vero actually works.

Every time a job is completed on Vero, a permanent signed record is created. Both sides confirm it. It links to every job before it. It cannot be edited. It belongs to the worker.

That is the entire idea. The rest is infrastructure to make it honest.

02 / How VERO works

Five steps. Two signatures. One permanent record.

  1. 01

    Join and get verified

    Workers and businesses verify their identity before anything else. No anonymous accounts, no fake history.

  2. 02

    Agree on the work

    Both sides agree in writing on what needs to be done. For paid work, the money is held in escrow so everyone knows it is real.

  3. 03

    Do the work

    A plumber fixes the tap. A designer delivers the logo. A developer ships the feature. Real work, with real people.

  4. 04

    Both sides sign

    When the job is done, both the worker and the client confirm it. Both signatures are required — neither side can do it alone.

  5. 05

    The record is yours forever

    A permanent, signed record is created. It links to your previous jobs. It cannot be edited, deleted, or taken away from you.

What a record looks like

Small. Signed. Permanent.

Each record carries the job description, both signatures, the date, an optional measurable detail, and a link to every record that came before it. Nothing is hidden. Nothing can be erased.

03 / Why you can trust it

A record that is hard to fake. By design.

Vero is designed so that neither workers nor businesses — and not even the platform itself — can quietly alter a record after it is created.

  • 01

    Both sides must sign

    Every record requires a signature from the worker and the client. One side cannot create history without the other.

  • 02

    Money held in escrow

    For paid work, funds are committed before the job starts. The worker knows the money is real. The client knows it is safe.

  • 03

    Disputes are handled fairly

    If something goes wrong, there is a clear path — direct, then mediated, then reviewed. Every step is logged.

  • 04

    History cannot be rewritten

    Each record is linked to the one before it. Nothing can be quietly edited after the fact.

  • 05

    Fraud is actively caught

    Identity, payment, and behaviour signals are cross-checked continuously. Bad actors are removed.

  • 06

    Reputation has real reasons

    Your standing comes from show-up rate, repeat clients, completion record, and dispute history — not just one number.

04 / Your work record

A history you own. One that goes wherever you go.

Your Vero profile is built from completed jobs, not self-descriptions. Every entry is signed by the person who hired you. You can carry it anywhere.

  • Job historyEvery verified job you have completed, in order, with both signatures and the original brief.
  • What you builtWhat was made, fixed, shipped, or delivered. Linked to the output when both sides agree to share it.
  • Trust over timeHow your standing has grown. The direction matters more than any single number.
  • Client endorsementsSigned endorsements from people you have worked for. Weighted by their own track record.
  • Portable credentialsYour record can be exported and shared outside Vero. It belongs to you, not the platform.
  • Verified specialisationsMarks for specific skills — earned through completed work, not self-selected from a list.

05 / How AI helps

AI checks the work. It does not replace the worker.

AI inside VERO does one job: verify that what was claimed actually happened. It does not write profiles, manufacture reviews, or influence who gets hired.

  • 01

    Checks that completed deliverables match what was agreed at the start

  • 02

    Matches workers to jobs based on their actual record — not just keywords in a bio

  • 03

    Detects fake signatures, duplicate accounts, and coordinated gaming

  • 04

    Turns long project histories into clear, readable summaries

  • 05

    Weighs signals so your reputation reflects real work, not just activity
