Who this is for

Small team or solo. You deserve the same protection.

Vero is not just for big companies with HR departments. It is built for the people who make one bad hire and feel it for months.

  • SMBs

    Growing teams that hire regularly.

    Studios, agencies, repair shops, clinics, retail stores. You hire often enough that one bad pick costs real money and time. Vero gives you a pool where every worker has a track record.

  • Solo clients

    One job. One person. Done right.

    You need a plumber, a designer, a weekend photographer. You do not want to guess. Hire from people whose past clients have already signed off on the work.

  • Cafés & households

    Local work deserves verified people.

    Baristas, cooks, household helpers, event staff. Vero treats small local work as seriously as a corporate contract — because the people doing it deserve that too.

Why Vero

Four things that change when the pool is verified.

  • See verified history, not a star rating.

    A 4.8 star rating tells you the last client was satisfied. A verified record tells you how many jobs were completed, what they involved, and whether any disputes were raised.

  • Escrow protects you from day one.

    Funds are committed before work starts and released only when both sides confirm completion. The platform fee is 5% — flat, visible, and below the market.

  • Disputes are handled fairly and on record.

    If something goes wrong, there is a clear three-step path — direct, mediated, then reviewed. Every step is logged. Nothing gets buried.

  • Rehire the people who worked out.

    Invite workers you have used before directly. Build a small verified network of people you trust — without starting from scratch every time.

How money works

Funded before the job starts. Released when both sides confirm it is done.

Escrow protects both sides. The worker knows the money is real. You know it is safe until the job is confirmed complete. No surprises.
