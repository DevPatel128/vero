# Vero. Proof of work, not posts about work.

LinkedIn shows what people claim. Vero shows what people did.

Every job you complete becomes a verified record on your profile. Signed by you. Signed by the person who hired you. Chained to the work that came before. Nobody — not even Vero — can edit it later.

> Launching in Bengaluru, Q3 2026. Workers join free.

## The problem

You need experience to get hired. You need to get hired to get experience. Most platforms ignore that loop. Vero closes it.

If you are a student, a beginner, a switcher, a skilled worker without a portfolio — the door is supposed to be hard to open. Vero opens it through small, real, verified work.

If you are a household, a café, a startup, an SMB — you spend hours screening unverified candidates. Vero gives you a worker whose record is already proof.

---

## How a Vero record is made

1. **You apply or get matched.** Local gigs, apprenticeships, paid work. Bengaluru first.
2. **You do the work.** No bidding war. No race to the bottom.
3. **The client confirms completion.** They sign. You sign.
4. **The record is minted.** Chained to your previous records. Public if you want it. Private if you don't.

That's it. A real verifiable thing you did. Forever yours.

## The protocol behind it

Vero records are written using **ALVED** — the Authentic Ledger of Validated Evolution Data. ALVED is an open spec, shared with RIE and Trove. Your work record is portable across products and apps that read the protocol. Learn more on [`/alved`](/alved).

## Frequently asked

### What kinds of work are on Vero at launch?
Home services, apprenticeships, kitchen + chef work, café + retail shifts, repair + maintenance, child care, elder care, content + design for SMBs, and local SMB project work. The full list is on the [for-businesses](/vero/for-businesses) page.

### Is my work record private?
You choose. Each record has a visibility flag — public, shared, or private. Public records are part of your public profile. Private records still count toward your trust score but are not visible.

### Can I take my Vero record with me?
Yes. Every record is exportable as JSON-LD signed by the original parties. You own it.

### Do I have to verify my ID to work on Vero?
For paid work, yes — via DigiLocker e-KYC. For unpaid apprenticeships, optional.

### How does Vero protect me from bad clients?
Escrow holds the money. Bad clients lose access. The trust graph cuts off repeat offenders.
