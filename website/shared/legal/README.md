## Rules
1. Never write here without citing the law.
2. Region pages take precedence over the master `privacy.md` where they apply.
3. Updates must include a "last updated" date.
4. Every change goes through legal review.
5. The grievance officer's contact details must be current.

See [`/docs/COMPLIANCE.md`](../../../docs/COMPLIANCE.md) for the matrix.
