# Go-to-Market

> Sources: Documents/9

## Growth philosophy

Growth through proof, progress, and social credibility — not paid advertising, virality gimmicks, or engagement-maximizing dark patterns. The core loop (`Documents/9`): a worker completes verified work → earns a permanent signed record → shares it → others recognize it as credible → new workers and businesses join → local density grows → more opportunities appear → the network becomes more valuable for everyone already in it.

## Launch market

Bengaluru, not India broadly. Five priority zones: Whitefield, HSR Layout, Koramangala, Sarjapur, Electronic City. Rule stated in the source: density beats reach — a working trust network in five zones is worth more than a thin presence across many cities (`Documents/9`). See `07_BUSINESS/MARKET.md` for why Bengaluru.

## Acquisition channels

1. **Campus ambassador program.** One ambassador per campus initially (top engineering, design, business schools). Rewarded on the quality of users brought in (retention, verified work completed), not signup count. Best ambassadors earn a "Verified Ambassador" badge that becomes part of their own VERO record.
2. **Startup and creative community activation.** Partnerships with co-working spaces (WeWork, Bhive, 91springboard named as examples in the source), founder-led content, community events (hiring panels, AMAs), targeted outreach to early-stage founders who cannot afford recruiters.
3. **Worker word-of-mouth.** Shareable record link/PDF/badge, milestone share-cards (first verified job, trust-tier growth), referral recognition tied to referred-user quality, not volume.

## Referral philosophy

Rewards are based on whether referred users build a real track record, not on referral count. A worker referring 10 people who never complete a job earns nothing; a worker referring 2 who each complete 5 signed jobs earns significant recognition (`Documents/9`).

## Local network strategy

City- and neighborhood-level reputation layers: city reputation pages ("Top culinary contributors in Bengaluru"), neighborhood density thresholds so each launch zone is self-sustaining, and local trust signals (repeat hires within a neighborhood weighted more meaningfully than scattered jobs nationally).

## What VERO will not do for growth

Explicitly ruled out in `Documents/9`: fake scarcity ("only 3 spots left"), spammy re-engagement notifications, dark patterns (hidden fees, forced upsells), fabricated metrics ("10,000 users joined this week" if untrue), and paid placement in search/ranking. This matches `01_PRINCIPLES/PRINCIPLES.md` rules on no dark patterns and no manufactured urgency.
