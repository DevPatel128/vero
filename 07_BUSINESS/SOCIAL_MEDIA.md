# Social Media & Content Operating System

> Status: Review · Owner: Dev Patel · Version: 1 · Last updated: 2026-09-28
> Source: Wolf v3 framework, `07_BUSINESS/SOCIAL_MEDIA.md` (imported unchanged apart from this header)

## Purpose
Turn product understanding into platform-native distribution, trust, demand, community, conversion and learning.

PRODUCT → VALUE → STORY → DISTRIBUTION → AUDIENCE → CONVERSION → FEEDBACK → PRODUCT.

## X
Role: real-time conversation, founder voice, ideas, product/technical thinking, launches and community.
Formats: concise insights, threads, demos, selective build-in-public, replies and long-form Articles where useful.
Formula: Hook → insight → evidence → implication → conversation.
Do not rage-bait, fake controversy or manufacture engagement.

## LinkedIn
Role: professional trust, B2B credibility, thought leadership, hiring, partnerships and sales enablement.
Formats: original research, customer problems, case studies, founder POV, product demos and charts/carousels.
Formula: Problem → original insight → evidence → implication → discussion/CTA.
Use credible subject-matter experts and align topics to business objectives.

## Instagram
Role: visual identity, product desire, culture, demos, education and community.
Formats: Reels, carousels, Stories, product visuals, customer stories, visual explainers and authorized UGC.
Formula: Visual hook → one idea → proof/demo → takeaway → CTA.
Create natively rather than reposting unchanged.

## YouTube
Role: deep education, search/discovery, demos, trust and durable content library.
Formats: long-form explainers, tutorials, product walkthroughs, case studies, documentaries, interviews and Shorts.
System: Idea → audience → promise → title/thumbnail → opening → value → retention → CTA → follow-up.
Optimize appeal, engagement and satisfaction. Use retention, watch time and audience data. Do not artificially stretch videos.

## Substack
Role: owned audience, deep research, recurring relationship, community and subscription layer.
Formats: essays, research, product-building letters, case studies, podcast/video, Notes and collaborations.
Use recommendations, referrals, community and subscription mechanics.
Treat it as an owned relationship layer, not merely another feed.

## Product-feature selling
technical capability → user problem → user outcome → emotional payoff → proof → content angle → platform → CTA → conversion event.

Never market "feature exists" without explaining the problem and outcome.

## Audience ladder
Unknown → Viewer/Reader → Engaged → Follower → Subscriber → Visitor → Trial → Activated → Retained → Advocate.

## Content pillars
Select 3–7: problem education, product/solution, original research, founder POV, customer proof, category education, culture/building.

## Content portfolio
Reach / Trust / Conversion / Community / Authority.

## Quality gate
Truth, evidence, platform fit, usefulness, honest hook, human voice, visual clarity, appropriate CTA, brand fit, privacy and capability accuracy.

## Community routing
Classify meaningful interactions:
question, objection, bug, feature request, misconception, praise, use case, competitor mention, partnership opportunity, customer evidence.
Route product/security/customer issues into the appropriate system.

## Creator / UGC
Evaluate relevance, trust, authority, audience overlap, quality and compliance. Obtain rights. Never fabricate testimonials or endorsements.

## Launch campaign
Seed → Reveal → Launch → Proof → Sustain.

## Repurposing
Research → Substack/long-form → YouTube → LinkedIn → X → Instagram → Shorts/Reels/Notes.
Repurpose the idea, not the file. Every platform gets a native hook, visual, length and CTA.

## Experimentation
Each test records hypothesis, audience, format, hook, distribution, CTA, primary metric, guardrails, duration, cost and decision rule.
Test hooks, topics, formats, creators, packaging, timing, CTA and landing paths.

## Measurement
Awareness: reach/impressions.
Attention: watch time/retention/completion.
Trust: saves/shares/meaningful comments/repeat audience.
Acquisition: profile/site/email/subscriber growth.
Activation: signup/first-value event.
Business: conversion, revenue, CAC, margin, retention.
Learning: objections, feature requests, research questions.

## Attribution
Use platform analytics, UTMs, landing analytics, first/last touch, assisted conversion, qualitative source questions and cohorts. Acknowledge imperfect attribution.

## AI content governance
AI may research, cluster questions, draft, repurpose and analyze.
Human review for sensitive, legal, controversial, customer-specific or brand-defining statements.
Never fabricate testimonials, results, customers, quotes, screenshots, endorsements or capabilities.

## Crisis
DETECT → VERIFY → CLASSIFY → ESCALATE → RESPOND → MONITOR → DOCUMENT → LEARN.

## Master prompt
"Read PRODUCT.md, THESIS.md, RESEARCH.md, CUSTOMER.md, GTM.md and METRICS.md. Identify audience, problem, outcome, evidence, capability, objections, platform, objective and conversion event. Create platform-native content. Do not invent facts, metrics, testimonials or capabilities. Produce 3 materially different hooks, visual direction and CTA. Run claim/privacy/brand audit."

## Principle
Do not chase attention. Earn attention by making the product, research and ideas worth sharing.
