Growth experiments require:
hypothesis, segment, intervention, metric, guardrail, expected result, cost, duration, evidence and decision rule.

Avoid growth mechanisms that compromise trust or user control.

## Applied in this repo

No growth experiment has run yet — `website/site` is a pre-launch waitlist with no user base to experiment against. `GTM.md` describes the planned go-to-market motion (referral program, ambassador program per `Documents/13`'s phase plan); those become growth experiments, in this file's format, once there is traffic to test against. One guardrail already decided, not merely proposed: `01_PRINCIPLES/PRINCIPLES.md` #6 ("No dark patterns") rules out any growth mechanism that would compromise it, regardless of measured lift.
