## Strategy Choice Cascade
1. Winning aspiration
2. Where to play
3. How to win
4. Required capabilities
5. Management systems

Strategy is mutually reinforcing choices, not a wish list.

Every strategic choice records evidence, alternatives, trade-offs, capabilities, cost, risk, falsifier and review date.

Business Model Canvas + Value Proposition Canvas are hypotheses to validate, not declarations of truth.

## Applied in this repo

No formal Strategy Choice Cascade has been run and recorded for Vero — `PRODUCT.md`, `THESIS.md` and `BUSINESS-MODEL.md` together carry the de facto strategy (winning aspiration: verified proof-of-work becomes the default hiring signal; where to play: Bengaluru gig/freelance work first; how to win: dual-signature records no competitor requires) but not in this cascade's explicit form. Running it formally, with evidence and a falsifier per choice, is unscoped work — flag as a candidate `08_DECISIONS/` entry once there is a strategy review to hold it for; not invented here.
