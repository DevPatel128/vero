## Market definition

VERO operates in what it calls "verified proof-of-work identity infrastructure" — a category the source material describes as not fully existing yet, distinct from gig marketplaces, professional networks, and home-services platforms (`Documents/7`). This framing is the company's own positioning, not an independently validated market taxonomy.

## Competitive landscape

Full comparison: `Documents/7. How VERO Beats the Competition.md`. Summary:

| Competitor | Category | What it gets wrong per VERO's positioning | Commission (as stated, unverified) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fiverr / Upwork | Bidding gig marketplace | Race to the bottom on price; reputation not portable; weak verification | ~20% / ~20%+ |
| LinkedIn | Professional network | Entirely self-reported, no verification | N/A (not commission-based) |
| Urban Company | Home-services marketplace | Platform-owned worker profiles; narrow category scope | Up to ~25% |
| Word of mouth | Informal | Works, but limited to existing personal networks | N/A |

VERO's stated position is that it has no direct competitor building the same dual-signature portable-record mechanism. This has not been independently checked against a broader competitive landscape search (no such search is recorded in this repo).

## Why India, why Bengaluru

`Documents/9`: Bengaluru chosen for startup-ecosystem density, a large young workforce matching VERO's target user profile, tech literacy, and tight word-of-mouth networks. This is reasoned, not independently benchmarked against alternative first-market candidates.
